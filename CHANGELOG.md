# v0.3

Introduced foundational reframe: the Stack's layers are dimensions of agency, not merely domains of governance. The Stack now explicitly maps the conditions under which individual and collective agency is possible, sustainable, and contestable.

Sharpened all six layer questions from descriptive to contestable — from *what is this domain* to *what is at stake here, and what does autonomy require of it*.

Reframed Layer 4 around contestability, fluidity, and revision: the concern is not who holds authority but whether authority can be challenged, revised, and undone.

Elevated Layer 5 from condition to process: knowledge formation as an ongoing collective act, vulnerable to epistemic warfare and linguistic capture.

Deepened Layer 6 from sustainability to relational foundation: care as the substrate of collective agency, not merely its support condition.

Extended Layer 2 to account for institutional capture and weaponization as failure modes alongside design.

Extended Layer 3 to include linguistic and semantic displacement as a contestability threat operating before formal decisions are made.

Integrated an extended research corpus — eighteen months of applied analysis — into the architecture. Patterns, concepts, and provocations from that corpus now ground the framework in observed conditions.

Added legitimacy note: when legitimacy is absent, it signals that one or more dimensions of agency have been foreclosed.

# v0.2

Reframed the Autonomy Stack as a governance operating system and reference architecture, clarifying its role as a shared logic for coordination rather than a blueprint or policy program.

Shifted from a hierarchical reading of layers to layers as reference axes, enabling multi-dimensional analysis without prescribed sequencing or priority.

Restored and clarified the project’s core purpose: advancing autonomy through better governance systems that connect rather than colonize.

Reworked structure and language to emphasize orientation, motion, and interdependence, replacing static or defensive framing.

Integrated patterns, signals, and modules as complementary instruments within the same reference frame.

Stabilized conceptual scope by deferring how-to guidance, implementation detail, and adoption mechanics to future versions.

v0.2 marks the transition from exploratory assembly to a coherent, emotionally grounded operating logic that can support future growth without premature hardening.
