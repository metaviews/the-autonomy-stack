The oligarchy funds far-right populism that claims to oppose the ruling class. This creates a paradox where capital backs the movement most likely to erode public services, yet directs public anger downwards toward immigrants rather than upward at the capitalist class.

### PATTERN CANDIDATE
Oligarchic Co-optation of Populist Protest
Phenomenon: Capital owners fund right-wing populist movements to neutralize left-wing alternatives by capturing anti-system sentiment. Context: Crisis of liberal legitimacy and wealth concentration. Forces: Need to protect property relations while appearing to challenge the status quo. Consequence: Consolidation of wealth and erosion of collective institutions while masking inequality as cultural grievance.

### CONCEPT
Possessive Individualism
Definition: A political theory (Macpherson) where liberal democracy is founded on the assumption that individuals are self-owning property holders. It treats society as a marketplace prioritizing transactions over collective obligation, rendering poverty a personal failure rather than systemic issue.
*Source note: Reference to C.B. Macpherson, The Political Theory of Possessive Individualism.*
*Connection: Foundation of Layer 4 (Decision Systems) and Layer 2 (Commons & Institutions) failures.*

**Economy of Care**
Definition: An alternative economic system prioritizing collective well-being, care labor, and public goods allocation over market-driven accumulation. It requires rejecting automation-for-profit in favor of technology serving shared prosperity.
*Source note: Section on "The Alternative: An Economy of Care."*
*Connection: Proposes a new operating mode for Layer 6 (Human Capacity & Care).*

### PROVOCATION
Right-Wing Framing Blocks Disagreement
The Stack principle "disagreement is a design condition" fails when power dynamics define political rivals as existential threats rather than adversaries. The right’s demonization of the left is strategic; if the left offers clear justice, the reactionary facade crumbles. Thus, maintaining the status quo requires preventing the *legibility* of leftist viability through media manipulation.
*Source note: Section on "The Right’s Insecurity and the Left’s Strength."*
*Connection: Challenges the Stack’s capacity for contestation when authority actively prevents the visibility of alternatives.*
