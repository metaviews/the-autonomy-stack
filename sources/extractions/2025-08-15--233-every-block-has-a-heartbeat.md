### PATTERN CANDIDATE
**Title:** Block Rhythm Mobilization for Civil Protection
**Phenomenon:** Rapid deployment of hyper-local social networks to disrupt state enforcement operations without direct physical conflict.
**Context:** Emergent in high-surveillance residential zones where formal legal recourse is insufficient or too slow for immediate threat.
**Forces:** Pre-existing trust ties (vendors, families, faith groups) combined with accessible low-tech resources.
**Consequences:** High short-term resilience in protecting vulnerable people, but risks long-term escalation of state counter-measures if the opacity of the network remains unregulated.
**Source Note:** Case study of "La Fantasma" organizing a sweep disruption using music, fake breakdowns, and smoke in El Paso.
