*2025-07-21*

## SIGNAL
**Title:** State Design Criminalizes Basic Human Survival
**Synthesized Content:** A shift where routine existence—sleeping, sharing food, digital connectivity—is redefined as criminal behavior by structural design rather than individual error. Survival becomes a skill that is systematically penalized.
**Source Note:** Lyric states the system is working "just fine" while punishing the poor and keeping the rich in line.
**Cross-reference note:** Also logged as PATTERN CANDIDATE: Administrative Legibility as Persecution Mechanism.

## PATTERN CANDIDATE
**Title:** Administrative Legibility as Persecution Mechanism
**Synthesized Content:** When systems require total visibility to operate, acts of care and subsistence become reportable data points triggering automated or bureaucratic enforcement, converting neighbors to threats.
**Phenomenon:** Mutual aid networks and personal sharing flagged as security threats.
**Context:** Emerges in high-surveillance urban environments and digital governance systems.
**Forces:** Risk algorithms, asset protection incentives, zero-tolerance policing, legal frameworks favoring property over person.
**Consequences:** Mutual aid networks collapse under regulatory scrutiny, isolating individuals from support and eroding community resilience.
**Source Note:** Song describes food growing and sharing supplies being flagged as threats in administrative offices.
**Cross-reference note:** Also logged in STACK MATERIAL: Stacking Layer 3 and 4.

## CONCEPT
**Title:** Framework: The Outlaw by Design Model
**Proposed Definition:** A governance lens viewing legal status not as a reflection of behavior, but as a structural output of system design. In this model, criminality is distributed according to access to resources and surveillance exposure.
**Source Note:** Based on the issue subtitle "In a surveillance society we're all outlaws" and lyrics describing systemic design.
**Cross-reference note:** Connects to STACK MATERIAL: Decision Systems & Authority.
