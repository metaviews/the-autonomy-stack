### CONCEPT
**Cultural Annexation vs. Political Annexation**
A distinct framework for describing sovereignty loss without legal transfer. Defined as the absorption of another system's political culture, narratives, and imagination through media and informal channels. Unlike legal annexation, it feels like "familiarity" and "normalcy," making resistance difficult because the shift occurs in the "mind" before institutions formally change.
*Connection to Stack: Relates to Sovereignty (Layer 2) and Legibility (Layer 3).*

### CONCEPT
**Sanitized Branding**
An organizational logic where institutional identity is curated to avoid controversy rather than represent members' complexities. It treats human nuance and "messy, human voices" as liability in favor of a unified, marketable front.
