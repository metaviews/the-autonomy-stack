### CONCEPT
**Gigerenzer’s Smart Heuristics Theory Of Emotional Intelligence**
*   **Definition:** A framework viewing gut feelings not as irrational errors but as evolved, efficient shortcuts for decision-making under uncertainty. Emotions are allies, not bugs.
*   **Lexicon Note:** Connects to Layer 6 (Human Capacity) and challenges Layer 4 (Decision Systems) models that prioritize cold calculation over intuition.
*   **Cross-reference:** Also logged as STACK MATERIAL: Layer 3 Metrics Attempting To Legibilize Internal Feelings.
*   **Relevance:** Supports the argument that eliminating emotion from systems is scientifically invalid, suggesting systems should be designed to recognize heuristic value.

**Lakoff’s Embodied Cognition Theory Of Metaphorical Reasoning**
*   **Definition:** A framework stating all reasoning is metaphorical and grounded in bodily experience. No "clean" logic exists independent of values and feeling.
*   **Lexicon Note:** Challenges Layer 4’s assumption that laws/procdures can be neutral. Reasoning is always structured by metaphorical feeling (safety, threat).
*   **Cross-reference:** Also logged as STACK MATERIAL: Layer 3 Metrics Attempting To Legibilize Internal Feelings.

### PROVOCATION
**True Authority Requires Emotional Understanding Not Pure Logic**
*   **Insight:** Challenges the Stack’s traditional reliance on rational-legibility for legitimacy. Cognitive science shows "pure logic" is a fantasy used to mask bias.
*   **Tension:** Authority based on "data" (ERT) looks rational but is emotionally blind, whereas authority grounded in listening requires embodied emotional capacity.
*   **Implication:** If reason is always emotional, then excluding emotion (via ERT) destroys the very basis of valid authority.
*   **Cross-reference:** Also logged as STACK MATERIAL: Layer 4 Authority Delegating Legitimacy To Opaque Data.
