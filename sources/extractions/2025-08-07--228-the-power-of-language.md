### SIGNAL

**Fragmenting Shared Meaning Amidst Proliferating Dialects**
Shared reality is fragmenting as language diversifies rather than unites. Despite new expressive capacity, common understanding decreases. This creates tension between local dialects and the ability to coordinate collective action via shared text.
Source: Metaviews salon participants debated boundaries and viral nature of language, noting common meaning feels increasingly rare.

### PATTERN CANDIDATE

**Viral Contagion of Conceptual Frames Reshapes Society**
Language behaves like a biological virus, infecting people, mutating, and spreading beyond original intent. This dynamic constructs reality rather than merely describing it. Ignoring this risk allows unexamined power structures to scaffold perception automatically.
Source: Salon discussion described language as inhabiting users and spreading mutability across digital platforms.
