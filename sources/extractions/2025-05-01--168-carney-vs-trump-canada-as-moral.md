### PATTERN CANDIDATE

**Title:** Weaponized Trade Elicits Defensive Coalition Building Dynamics
**Synthesized Content:** When a dominant power uses economic levers (tariffs, sanctions) for political coercion, intermediate states form defensive blocs to mitigate exposure. This phenomenon produces a fragmentation of global trade architecture into more localized, resilient alliances prioritizing reliability over historical loyalty or unipolar convenience. Ignoring this leads to increased vulnerability and supply chain shocks.
**Source Note:** Describes how Trump's tariffs and sanctions force countries to search for new partners who are "reliable, rational, and rules-based."
