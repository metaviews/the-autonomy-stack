# 161: Research as a Revolutionary Act

**Building a science that authoritarianism cannot corrupt**  
*2025-04-22*

### SIGNAL

**Regime Erases Capacity To Discern Information Structure**
Authoritarianism attacks the capacity for the public to distinguish evidence, interpret meaning, and question power—not just specific scientific findings. This erodes the conditions for informed collective agency.
*Source note: Paraphrase of text describing the campaign against public understanding, academic freedom, and the capacity to question power as an attack on how knowing happens.*
Cross-reference: None.

### PATTERN CANDIDATE

**Epistemic Assault Targets Method Instead Of Particular Truths**
**Phenomenon:** Oppressive regimes prioritize eroding public cognitive tools for verification over falsifying specific data points.
**Context:** Emerging in democratic backsliding scenarios where institutions like universities face politically appointed control.
**Forces:** Ideological systems rooted in obedience fear the inherent uncertainty and independence of inquiry.
**Consequences:** Long-term inability for the public to make informed decisions or hold authority contestable.
Cross-reference: Also logged as SIGNAL: Regime Erases Capacity To Discern Information Structure.

### CONCEPT

**Inquiry Without Permission Research Beyond State Approval**
The practice of conducting scientific or social inquiry without requiring sanction from state institutions or established funding bodies. Treats knowledge creation as a civic right embedded in community life.
**Definition:** Research as participatory, public, and rooted in mutual care rather than institutional security.
*Source note: Paraphrase of text defining resistance as building practices that do not require permission or institutional affiliation.*
Cross-reference: Also logged as STACK MATERIAL: Distributed Knowledge Infrastructure.

**Epistemic Colonialism Hierarchical Systems Enforcing Knowledge Control**
Conceptual framework describing how dominant groups exclude marginalized perspectives (Indigenous, Black, queer) by enforcing extraction, erasure, and control rather than collaboration or shared authority.
**Definition:** Knowledge hierarchies that validate only certain ways of knowing while dehumanizing others.
*Source note: Paraphrase of text calling for rejecting colonial hierarchies of where Indigenous science and marginalized studies are essential.*
Cross-reference: None.
