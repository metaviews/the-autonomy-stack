### SIGNAL
**Silicon Shield Emerges as Defense Metric**

The discourse regarding Taiwan security now prioritizes production capacity (semiconductors) over troop numbers. The "silicon shield" metaphor frames economic indispensability as a primary defense mechanism, suggesting state authority relies increasingly on industrial leverage versus territorial control. This signals a shift where Material Base assets double as strategic deterrents.

**Connections:** Links Layer 1 (Material Base) to Layer 4 (Decision Systems). Challenges existing lexicon by introducing "economic deterrence" as a governance state.

### PROVOCATION
**Material Monopoly Threatens Contested Authority Principle**

The Autonomy Stack principle "authority must be contestable" conflicts with "Silicon Shield" dynamics where the producer holds a monopoly. Contestation becomes suicidal rather than transformative. If resilience depends on a single point of failure, "legibility" of the attacker is insufficient to prevent self-harm. This suggests "resilience over elegance" may require intentional redundancy to prevent material hegemony.
