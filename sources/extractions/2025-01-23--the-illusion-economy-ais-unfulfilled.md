## CONCEPT

**The "Illusion Economy" Framework**
An economic and governance model where value is generated through circulating narratives of potential rather than tangible production. Governance within this system relies on the continuous deferral of value extraction, making accountability structurally impossible until the bubble bursts or credibility evaporates completely due to unsustainable interest costs.
*Source Note: Title and subtitle framing of the newsletter.*

**The AI Debt Spiral Dynamic**
A cyclical dynamic where unfulfilled technological promises compound the cost of trust and legitimacy rather than resolving them. Each new promise adds interest to the principal debt of credibility, requiring larger future claims to sustain current authority levels without actually delivering the foundational material or social benefits previously guaranteed.
