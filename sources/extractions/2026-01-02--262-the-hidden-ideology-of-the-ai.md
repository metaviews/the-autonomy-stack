# 262: The Hidden Ideology of AI

**Why Boosters and Doomsters Share the Same Dangerous Assumptions**  
*2026-01-02*

---

### SIGNAL
**Shared Ideology Ranks Human Worth by Cognitive Performance**
The public debate between AI boosters and doomsters obscures a deeper consensus: both assume human value depends on cognitive output and efficiency. This consensus naturalizes exclusion by framing non-optimal cognition as technical debt. It suggests a systemic failure to recognize care, relational intelligence, or uneven capacity as valid forms of contribution. The current discourse cannot produce wisdom because it begins from exclusion.

### PATTERN CANDIDATE
**Sorting Logics Prioritize Metrics Over Care Dynamics**
**Phenomenon:** Governance systems increasingly define legitimacy through measurable performance and ranked intelligence rather than relational support.
**Context:** Tech policy, labor valuation, disability inclusion, and AI ethics.
**Forces:** Optimization logic, efficiency metrics, and industrial-era thinking repackaged as technological progress.
**Consequences:** Legitimizes exclusion and erases relational intelligence, rendering those who cannot meet metrics as surplus. Ignoring this pattern entrenches ableist hierarchies and blocks pathways for pluralistic participation.

### CONCEPT
**Politics of Sorting Versus Politics of Care**
**Definition:** Two opposing governance paradigms. "Politics of Sorting" values hierarchy, ranking, and competitive performance to determine legitimacy. "Politics of Care" values interdependence, support, and relational integrity.
**Connection:** Connects to Stack Material on Human Capacity and Care. Provides a vocabulary for analyzing how authority distributes resources and dignity.
**Source:** Explicit binary presented as the central tension in the text.
**Related:** Also logged as Stack Material: AI Legitimacy Defined by Care Expansion.

**Human Difference as Technical Debt**
**Definition:** A metaphorical framework where cognitive or physical variance is treated as a system error or cost to be managed, rather than a feature of the population.
**Connection:** Challenges Legibility & Metrics layer's assumption that all variance is measurable/optimizable without loss of value.
**Source:** Used in text to describe booster logic regarding human inefficiency.
**Related:** Also logged as Concept Framework.

**Intelligence as Destiny**
**Definition:** A cultural assumption that individual or group worth is predetermined by cognitive output and efficiency capabilities.
**Connection:** Links Knowledge & Intelligence to Decision Systems. Suggests that when intelligence defines destiny, authority becomes rigid and exclusionary.
**Source:** Core theoretical claim of the newsletter issue.

### PROVOCATION
**Critics Reinforce Ability Hierarchies Even When Opposing AI**
This issue challenges the assumption that critiques of technology are inherently progressive. If AI critics defend a "sacred image" of human intelligence that excludes neurodivergence or non-standard cognition, they replicate the ableist hierarchies they claim to oppose. By policing the boundary of "real" intelligence, they narrow who qualifies as human or worthy.
