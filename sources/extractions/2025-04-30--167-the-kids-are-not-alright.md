**From Isolation to Insurrection: The Political Vacuum Fueling Gen Z Extremism**

---

### SIGNAL

**Youth Shift From Apathy To Violent Engagement**
Gen Z political activity is not declining but transforming into high-intensity extremism due to perceived exclusion from traditional power structures.
**Source Note:** Newsletter observes polling data showing high support for far-right figures among young men despite general youth political anxiety.
**Also logged as:** PATTERN CANDIDATE (Exclusion Leads To Digital Recruitment And Violence)

### PATTERN CANDIDATE

**Exclusion Leads To Digital Recruitment And Violence**
Systemic inability to confer agency on demographics drives vulnerable individuals toward decentralized networks promising immediate community and purpose.
**Context:** Modern democracies combined with social media infrastructure where anonymity enables radicalization.
**Forces:** Perceived powerlessness, algorithmic targeting, and untreated mental health strains create entry points.
**Consequence:** Legitimacy crisis for state authority and increased physical/digital threats from mobilized groups.
**Source Note:** Newsletter identifies the 764 network specifically as leveraging exclusion to mobilize violence against the status quo.
**Also logged as:** SIGNAL (Youth Shift From Apathy To Violent Engagement)
