# Government of the Inept?

**Examining Trump’s Cabinet Picks**  
*2024-11-14*

---

### SIGNAL
**Loyalty Norms Supplanting Technical Expertise in Appointments**
Governance is shifting its operating criteria from functional qualification to political allegiance, specifically within agencies governing security and justice. The administrative selection process now prioritizes alignment over established professional standards, creating a visible break from institutional norms. This shift signals that the state is treating internal authority not as a resource to be maintained, but as a position to be secured through personal fidelity. The risk is not merely inefficiency, but a fundamental redefinition of state power that relies on trust in leaders rather than institutions.
*Source Note: Analysis of the Trump administration's Cabinet selections where appointments are valued for defending the administrator rather than technical fitness.*

### PATTERN CANDIDATE
**Efficiency Agencies as Mechanisms for Political Purging**
Phenomenon: Creation of new administrative bodies framed as streamlining or efficiency measures to consolidate internal control and filter dissent.
Context: Appears during political transitions where incumbents seek to restructure the bureaucracy to enforce ideological homogeneity.
Forces: Corporate management practices applied to public systems, combined with executive mandates that prioritize loyalty over statutory roles.
Consequences: If engaged, public administration loses stability and institutional memory. If ignored, the underlying shift in power remains opaque until trust collapses.
