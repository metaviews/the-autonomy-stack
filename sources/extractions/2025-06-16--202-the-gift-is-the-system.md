### SIGNAL
**Care Economy Demonstrates Functional Infrastructure Status**
A localized community operates without market mechanisms, demonstrating that care and gifting constitute the functional infrastructure of daily life rather than supplementary social activities.
**Source Note:** Newsletter describes daily routines where tasks, food, and repairs are exchanged via need rather than contract, establishing systemic reliability without money.
**Cross-Reference:** Also logged as STACK MATERIAL and PATTERN CANDIDATE regarding non-market governance.

### PATTERN CANDIDATE
**Implicit Reciprocity Replaces Formal Labor Exchange**
Labor and resource allocation rely on distributed social memory and long-term reciprocity rather than immediate transactional exchange, creating resilience through obligation rather than enforcement.
**Source Note:** Community members trade childcare, repairs, and food based on remembered past exchanges rather than explicit agreement or tracking, trusting the network balances over time.
**Cross-Reference:** Also logged in STACK MATERIAL under Human Capacity & Care.
