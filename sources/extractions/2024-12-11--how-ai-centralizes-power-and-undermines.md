### SIGNAL

**AI Ideology Masks Centralization of Authority**
Artificial intelligence is marketed as neutral efficiency while functioning as an ideological project to consolidate authority in centralized structures. The narrative prioritizes system ubiquity over the question of whether these systems should exist.

### PATTERN CANDIDATE

**Problem Redefinition Enforces AI Dependency Cycles**
AI expansion redefines existing problems so that only AI can solve them, creating a cycle of technological dependence. This process privatizes collective decision-making capacities and places them under centralized control regardless of root causes.
**Context:** Systems used for hiring, medical diagnosis, and loan approvals.
**Forces:** Interests of developers and deployers; the prevailing narrative of technological inevitability.
**Consequences:** Erosion of human agency and collective autonomy; replacement of common capacities with centralized services.
**Source Note:** Section "The False Necessity of AI" discussing the cycle of dependence and enclosure.
