**Consequences:** Integrating these perspectives increases systemic legibility and resistance to corruption; ignoring them entrenches governance by deception.
*Source: Analysis of Diggers, Levellers, Goldman, Heumann, and Indigenous governance as movements built on seeing through systemic lies.*

### CONCEPT

**Truth as Praxis**
**Definition:** A governance mode where exposing deception is secondary to acting on lived truth; legitimacy is derived from the ability to act on reality rather than simply naming it.
**Connection:** Extends Stack Principle "Legibility over expertise" by requiring action, not just visibility.
*Source: Description of how a movement would build alternatives based on lived truth rather than just exposing lies.*

**Neurodivergent Political Movement**
**Definition:** A governance structure specifically designed around the capacities and needs of neurodivergent individuals, treating cognitive difference as a political asset rather than a deficit.
**Connection:** Challenges Layer 6 (Human Capacity) by redefining "capacity" away from neurotypical standards.
*Source: Proposal to organize a political movement around the needs and values of neurodivergent people.*
**Also logged as PATTERN CANDIDATE: Marginalized cognition as systemic truth-telling mechanism**

**Indigenous Governance Systems**
**Definition:** Models of authority characterized by distributed power, cognitive diversity, and consensus-based decisions, specifically rooted in long-term thinking and reciprocal care.
**Connection:** Provides historical evidence for Layer 4 (Decision Systems) alternatives to Western statehood.
*Source: Reference to Indigenous governance systems across Turtle Island offering distributed authority and consensus.*

### PROVOCATION

**If deception is structural, does contestability fail?**
This issue challenges the Stack assumption that "authority must be contestable" by suggesting deception is not a bug but the core feature of the system. If the "game" is always rigged, standard contestation mechanisms may be insufficient to reveal or change reality, requiring a fundamental shift in epistemic capacity rather than just procedural reform.
*Source: The premise that the "surface is a lie" and seeing patterns in deception is the only path to clarity.*
