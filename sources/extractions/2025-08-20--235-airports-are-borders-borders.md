### PATTERN CANDIDATE
Using Infrastructure Glitches To Gain Legal Time
The phenomenon involves coordinated physical and digital interference with transportation logistics to create operational delays. The context is high-security state transport (deportation charters), where time is critical. The resistance creates friction (jamming gates, confusing lights, banners on carousels) to force legal review and prevent movement. Ignoring this pattern assumes infrastructure is static; engaging it reveals the airport as a site of tactical legal leverage.
**Source Note:** A coordinated group uses luggage belts, lights, and audio to delay a covert charter flight, allowing lawyers to intervene.

### CONCEPT
'Legal Leverage' Through Operational Delay
**Definition:** A strategy where slowing down bureaucratic or state machinery creates a time window for legal intervention, rather than attempting to stop the motion physically. Success is measured in "time bought" rather than total cancellation.
**Source Note:** The narrator notes "Charter pilots don't like minutes. Lawyers love them," framing time as the strategic resource.
