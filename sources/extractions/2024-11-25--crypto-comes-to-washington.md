### CONCEPT

**Concept: Financial Democracy Defined**
An ideologically charged term used to describe markets where investment access is open to the public. In practice, as observed in current discourse, it functions to mask wealth concentration. The narrative suggests ownership leads to control, but in reality, early capital holders dictate terms. Legitimizing this term without critical distinction risks accepting market outcomes as inherently democratic.
*Source Note: Text identifies "Financial Democracy" as a seductive narrative that obscures true nature of wealth concentration.*

**Concept: Cryptographic Authority Defined**
A form of governance where rules are embedded in code and executed programmatically rather than through human institutions. Unlike democratic law, this authority is opaque, technically inaccessible to laypeople, and immutable unless hard-coded changes occur. It claims neutrality but often encodes specific market biases of its developers. It operates as a privatized layer of state power without the requirement of public consent.
