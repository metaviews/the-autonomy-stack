## STACK MATERIAL

**Title:** AI Proprietary Signals Replace Public Economic Metrics
**Synthesized Content:** Governance shifts from measuring human conditions (wages, employment) to optimizing proprietary model signals (confidence, compliance), rendering public data a liability.
**Source Note:** BLS focuses on people while AI regime counts models, suggesting future policy will rely on AI-generated signals rather than human indicators.
**Relevant Layer:** 3 (Legibility & Metrics). Complicates existing framing by introducing non-public data sources as primary legibility tools.
**Cross-Reference:** Also logged as STACK MATERIAL (Layer 4): AI Models Become Primary Decision-Making Authority.

**Title:** AI Models Become Primary Decision-Making Authority
**Synthesized Content:** Central banks and governments rely on same AI-generated signals to guide decisions, bypassing democratic oversight or external verification, creating a closed loop.
**Source Note:** Future economy described as one where policy is driven by proprietary models protecting status quo rather than public data.
**Relevant Layer:** 4 (Decision Systems & Authority). Contradicts principle of "authority must be contestable" by embedding decision power in black-box models.

**Synthesized Content:** Positions that serve no real purpose except to sustain the illusion that the economy is functioning, requiring performance over utility.
**Source Note:** Large percentage of modern employment described as nonsense, paper-pushing, and productivity theater without real purpose.

**Title:** The Ghost Economy Model
**Synthesized Content:** Post-labor system where work continues without dignity and productivity grows only in metrics, resembling automation and austerity rather than abundance.
**Source Note:** Description of future where appearance of activity replaces actual stability, and people are erased for trying to count the truth.
