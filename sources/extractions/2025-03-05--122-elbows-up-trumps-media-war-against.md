### CONCEPT
**Gramsci’s Cultural Hegemony Theory for Analysis**
*Proposed Definition:* A framework where a dominant group maintains power not by force alone, but by shaping the common sense and narratives of that society, making their rule appear natural and inevitable.
*Connection:* Extends Stack Layer 4 (Authority) by explaining how political subjugation occurs through narrative control rather than physical coercion.
*Source note:* Cited to explain how the U.S. can subjugate Canada economically by controlling the way Canadians think about their economic position.

**Ellul’s Propaganda Environment Overriding Factual Accuracy**
*Proposed Definition:* A condition where the volume and consistency of messaging create an environment of inevitability, where factual correctness is irrelevant compared to pervasive repetition.
*Connection:* Relates to Stack Layer 5 (Knowledge) and Layer 3 (Legibility), suggesting that "truth" is determined by the infrastructure of distribution rather than data quality.
*Source note:* Paraphrased note from the text stating Trump’s barrage does not need to be factually correct, only pervasive.

**McLuhan’s Medium Shapes Political Perception Directly**
*Proposed Definition:* The assertion that the method of communication (visual, immediate, emotional) dictates the nature of the political reality being constructed, often more than the content itself.
*Connection:* Critical for Stack Layer 3, as the medium determines how the populace perceives legitimacy and threat.
*Source note:* Cited to argue that Trump’s visual and emotional medium gives him an upper hand over Canada’s measured policy responses.

**Fanon’s Psychological Warfare Through Economic Dependency**
*Proposed Definition:* Tactics designed to instill systemic doubt in a population about their ability to resist, leading to internalized subjugation via economic framing.
*Connection:* Bridges Stack Layer 1 (Material Base) and Layer 6 (Human Capacity), showing how material threats translate to psychological submission.
