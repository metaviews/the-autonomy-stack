## SIGNAL

**Title:** Algorithmic Control Over Narrative Defines Emerging Power
**Content:** The control of digital algorithms that curate information is becoming the primary locus of authority, determining which political and cultural narratives are amplified or suppressed.
**Source Note:** Newsletter notes AI reshaping communication and questions who owns the algorithms that curate discourse.
**Cross-Reference:** Also logged as STACK MATERIAL: Knowledge & Intelligence (Layer 5).

## PATTERN CANDIDATE

**Title:** Pre-Political Narrative Building Precedes Legislative Power Access
**Content:** Successful political movements prioritize long-term cultural and intellectual dominance to establish "common sense" before seeking electoral mandates or policy changes.
**Context:** Observed in both contemporary left and right-wing strategies across media, academia, and digital platforms.
**Forces:** Decline of traditional institutional trust and the rise of fragmented digital subcultures.
**Consequences:** Legitimacy becomes contingent on cultural alignment and narrative control rather than legal mandate. If ignored, groups face obsolescence as they lose the battle for the public imagination.
**Cross-Reference:** Also logged as CONCEPT: Metapolitics.

## CONCEPT

**Title:** Metapolitics: Defining Ideological Conditions For Political Power
**Content:** A framework describing the battle to define the cultural, intellectual, and philosophical underpinnings of a society to make specific political powers possible.
**Connection:** Extends the Stack’s understanding of Layer 5 (Knowledge) and Layer 4 (Authority) by positing that power is contingent on prior narrative definition.

**Title:** Cultural Hegemony: Dominating Values Before Power Seizure
**Content:** A theory proposing that political change requires the dominance of progressive values in education, media, and intellectual spaces before seizing political office.
**Connection:** Relates to PATTERN CANDIDATE on pre-political narrative building; challenges the Stack's Material Base priority by prioritizing Idea Infrastructure.

**Title:** Overton Window: Shifting Terms Of Political Debate
**Content:** A mechanism for redefining the range of policies acceptable to the mainstream population before they are legally enacted.
**Connection:** Relates to Layer 3 (Legibility) by defining the boundaries of what the system can see and measure as "thinkable."
