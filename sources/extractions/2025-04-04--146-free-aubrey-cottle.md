### CONCEPT
**Aggressive Network Self-Defense**
*Definition:* A governance paradigm where defensive actors proactively identify threats, mislead adversaries, and engage in active disruption rather than waiting for attacks. It shifts cybersecurity from reactive maintenance to offensive prevention as a core element of national security.
*Origin:* Attributed here to security expert Neil Wyler (Grifter), circa 2005, repurposed here for state-level civil defense.
*Connections:* Connects to Layer 4 (Decision Systems) by redefining authorized force; requires new metrics in Layer 3 to measure "proactive threat density" rather than just "incident count."
*Cross-reference: Also logged under STACK MATERIAL (Layer 4/2 Proposal)*

**Political Prisoner of the Digital Age**
*Definition:* A classification for individuals persecuted not primarily for violence or theft, but for the visibility they generate, the resonance their actions have, and the threat they pose to power structures (e.g., Assange, Manning, Cottle).
*Origin:* Discourse emerging from cases comparing state punishment of digital dissidents to historical political imprisonment.
*Connections:* Complicates Layer 4 (Legitimacy) by challenging the binary of lawful vs. unlawful; suggests authority is illegitimate if it punishes visibility.
*Cross-reference: Also logged under PATTERN CANDIDATE (Criminalization of Exposure)*
