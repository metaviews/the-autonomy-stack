### PATTERN CANDIDATE

**Title:** Technology Driven Verification Paradox Creates Authority Vacuum
**Phenomenon:** AI tools democratize media creation while simultaneously destroying trust in verification.
**Context:** Appears in legal systems, journalism, and social governance where evidence validity is paramount.
**Forces:** Increasing accessibility of generative tools versus the slow, centralized process of human verification.
**Consequences:** Creates a vacuum where authority is contested by competing narratives rather than verified facts, necessitating new trust mechanisms.
**Source Note:** Derived from Filimowicz’s analysis of the dual role of deep fakes in creative liberation versus institutional destabilization.
**Cross-reference:** Also logged under SIGNAL.
