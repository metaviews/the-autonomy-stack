Context: Appears in national security or reshoring directives where immigration is restricted but production demand remains.
Forces: Driven by neoliberal labor suppression, nationalist rhetoric, and the commodification of human confinement.
Consequences: Deepens the cycle of disposability, criminalizes dissent, and erodes democratic rights in favor of production efficiency.
Source Note: Summary of the argument that immigrant workers are being replaced by prisoners to bypass labor standards and unionization.
