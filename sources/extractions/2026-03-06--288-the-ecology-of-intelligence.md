### SIGNAL
**Digital Tools Evolve Into Agentic Intelligence Ecologies**
Metaviews observes a functional shift where website features transform into autonomous capabilities rather than static features. This transition moves design focus from interface usability to information flow architecture between humans and machines. Governance becomes critical as agents begin interacting rather than serving single users. System behavior shifts from software execution to biological ecology, requiring new oversight mechanisms to prevent instability.

### PATTERN CANDIDATE
**Three-Layer Authority Model Stabilizes Agent Swarms**
Phenomenon is the tendency for unstructured agent networks to cascade errors and automate beyond accountability. Context appears in multi-human, multi-agent knowledge systems. Forces include speed of automation versus slowness of human review. Consequence is either collapse into noise or stability through distinct authority types. Engagement requires separating operational infrastructure management from epistemic judgment and participatory contribution.
*Source note: Definition of authority types in intelligence ecology to manage swarm behavior.*

### STACK MATERIAL
**Authority Architecture: Operational, Epistemic, Participatory**
**Layer 4 (Decision Systems), Layer 5 (Knowledge), Layer 1 (Material Base).**
Proposes separating stewardship roles: operational (infrastructure integrity), epistemic (knowledge value judgment), participatory (collaboration access). Suggests humans interact via personal agents carrying preferences and permissions. Notes material base integration where physical sensors and farmers act as nodes alongside digital tools. Challenges framing by embedding care and labor into technical architecture rather than treating them as separate from intelligence systems.
*Source note: Governance section describing interaction between humans, agents, and physical environments.*

### CONCEPT
**The Agency of Agents Framework**
Coined term describing participants as non-autonomous rulers of a digital world. Refers to agents existing within structured ecosystems with boundaries shaped by governance, editorial judgment, and institutional responsibility. Connects to Stack Principle of Legibility by bounding agency within human oversight. Defines agency not as isolation but as constrained capability within human judgment. Distinguishes from "autonomous AI" by mandating accountability structures.
*Source note: Phrase surfacing during reflection on governance needs for agentic computation.*

### PROVOCATION
**Stewardship Model Risks Centralizing Authority**
Text posits Metaviews as a "steward responsible for integrity" and maintaining operational responsibility. Contradicts Stack Principle that authority must be contestable and nothing is final. Implies a fixed arbiter exists to define system integrity without explicit mechanisms for challenging that arbiter. Questions where oversight on the steward resides within this proposed ecology. Potential risk of open-source label overriding centralization of architectural power.
*Source note: Description of Metaviews role maintaining operational responsibility and accountability.*
