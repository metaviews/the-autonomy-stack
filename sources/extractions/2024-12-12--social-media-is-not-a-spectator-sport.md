## STACK MATERIAL

**Layer Four: Authority Relies on Visibility**
Public judgment enforced by algorithms functions as a court system lacking legal recourse. Legitimacy is granted or revoked based on engagement velocity rather than rule adherence. This bypasses traditional authority checks and concentrates power in platform design.

**Layer Three: Trust Metrics Override Compliance**
In digital realms, perceived trustworthiness is measured through engagement and financial performance. Formal compliance becomes secondary to maintaining the illusion of integrity. Legibility is reduced to market reaction rather than policy adherence.
