## SIGNAL

**Title:** Algorithmic summarization obscures original historical sources
**Content:** Generative AI search results replace direct links to primary documents, stripping context and reducing history to an algorithm's fleeting interpretation rather than verifiable evidence.
**Source Note:** The text describes search engines as "bricked over," regurgitating summaries that obscure the existence of original materials.
**Cross-reference:** Also logged as STACK MATERIAL: Legibility & Metrics.

## PATTERN CANDIDATE

**Title:** Economic insolvency and AI erasure destroy accountability
**Content:** Media bankruptcy combined with AI optimization creates a vacuum where public records vanish overnight, allowing politicians to rewrite history without verification.
**Context:** Appears in digital news ecosystems facing financial collapse while integrating AI search tools.
**Forces:** Economic indifference to preservation, AI training biased toward power structures, lack of mandates for data retention.
**Consequences:** Resistance loses tools to contradict official narratives; truth becomes whatever power actors define.
**Cross-reference:** Also logged as STACK MATERIAL: Decision Systems & Authority.
