### SIGNAL
**Journalists Eliminated To Sever Info Chains**
Targeted elimination of media personnel functions as a strategic move to sever the link between observed events and public record. This shifts violence from physical territory control to narrative control, positioning the witness as an enemy combatant rather than a protected observer. Authority now depends on the capacity to erase testimony before it solidifies into evidence.

### PATTERN CANDIDATE
**Unverified Label Functions As Execution Order**
Phenomenon where states or authorities use sweeping, unverified allegations to bypass legal due process and legitimize lethal force. Occurs in conflict zones or high-surveillance environments where the accused cannot defend themselves. Driven by forces prioritizing information dominance over legal accountability. Consequences include normalized impunity and the destruction of institutional trust in official records. Ignoring this pattern allows power to operate without constraint.
*Source Note: Distillation of the text's argument regarding IDF claims that al-Sharif was a Hamas operative functioning as verdict without court.*

This entry challenges Layer 5 (Knowledge & Intelligence) and Layer 3 (Legibility & Metrics). Systematic removal of human sensors prevents the formation of shared situational awareness. When physical actors are targeted, the system becomes blind to its own effects. This extends the Autonomy Stack by highlighting that human safety is a prerequisite for systemic legibility. If the observer is unsafe, the observation cannot be trusted or trusted to survive.

### CONCEPT
**Narrative Occupation Replaces Physical Territorial Control**
The strategic replacement of physical occupation with a monopoly on historical memory and the official record. Distinction drawn between holding land temporarily and holding the narrative permanently. Requires definition because it reframes occupation not as geography but as information control. Connects to Stack Layer 5 (Knowledge) and Layer 4 (Authority). Implies that controlling history is a more enduring form of sovereignty than controlling land.
*Source Note: Derived from text assertion "In this logic, territory matters less than narrative. Occupying a land is temporary; occupying history is forever."*
