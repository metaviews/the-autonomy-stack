# The Myth of Corporate Meritocracy

**Do MMORPGs offer the solution?**  
*2024-11-21*

---

### SIGNAL

**HR Functions Reframed as Mechanisms of Corporate Control**
Organizational structures branded as 'Human Resources' are increasingly interpreted as gatekeeping mechanisms designed to protect corporate legal interests and enforce hierarchy rather than advocate for worker welfare.
*Source:* Analysis of HR departments as "Human Repression Department" that exist to ensure legal protection and manage public relations.
*Cross-reference:* Also logged as CONCEPT: The Human Repression Department Definition of HR Systems.

**Generational Shifts in Workforce Expectations Regarding Authority**
Demographic cohorts entering the workforce now display reduced willingness to accept opaque corporate hierarchies, demanding transparency and equitable distribution of value as conditions of labor.
*Source:* Observation that Gen Z and Millennials are less inclined to buy traditional corporate ladders and demand more transparency.
*Cross-reference:* None.

### PATTERN CANDIDATE

**Meritocracy Myths Masking Systemic Inequality in Workplaces**
Organizations frequently deploy the narrative of fairness to justify existing power structures, resulting in systems that reward compliance and obedience while masking barriers to genuine equity.
*Source:* Critique that the myth of meritocracy persists because it serves those at the top to mask systemic barriers under the guise of fairness.
*Cross-reference:* Also logged as CONCEPT: Meritocracy Myths as Ideological Justification of Authority.

### STACK MATERIAL

**Performance Reviews Create Zero-Sum Competition Among Employees**
(Layer 4: Decision Systems & Authority)
Evaluation systems designed to reward effort often devolve into subjective, political exercises that force workers into competitive isolation rather than fostering collaborative problem-solving.
*Source:* Discussion of performance reviews as exercises in subjective judgment where promotions remain scarce and workers are pitted against one another.
*Cross-reference:* Also logged as STACK MATERIAL: Performance Reviews Create Zero-Sum Competition Among Employees.

**Gamified Metrics Proposed for Transparent Career Progression Models**
(Layer 3: Legibility & Metrics)
A proposed structure uses quantifiable, trackable metrics for diverse contributions to replace arbitrary advancement, aiming to make labor value visible through clear progression systems.
*Source:* Proposal suggests transparent progression where career advancement is based on clear, quantifiable metrics tracked similarly to game experience levels.
*Cross-reference:* Also logged as CONCEPT: Skill Trees as Governance for Career Pathways.

**Inclusion Initiatives Often Reduced to Superficial Tokenism**
(Layer 6: Human Capacity & Care)
Diversity programs are frequently deployed as public relations tools that maintain structural inequalities, prioritizing statistics over material changes that empower marginalized groups.
*Source:* Note that diversity initiatives often amount to little more than PR strategies while maintaining structures that perpetuate inequality.
*Cross-reference:* None.

### CONCEPT

**The Human Repression Department Definition of HR Systems**
A redefinition of Human Resources describing the functional reality as gatekeeping corporate control rather than advocating for individual development, welfare, or internal equity.
*Source:* Article explicitly names HR 'Human Repression Department' to contrast stated purpose with operational reality of protecting company interests.
*Cross-reference:* Also logged as SIGNAL: HR Functions Reframed as Mechanisms of Corporate Control.

**Meritocracy Myths as Ideological Justification of Authority**
A framework identifying the belief in merit-based success as a legitimizing tool for inequality, where the appearance of fairness conceals the maintenance of power.
*Source:* Description of meritocracy as a myth serving those at the top to justify inequality and mask systemic barriers.
*Cross-reference:* Also logged as PATTERN CANDIDATE: Meritocracy Myths Masking Systemic Inequality in Workplaces.

**Skill Trees as Governance for Career Pathways**
A model utilizing branching development paths (derived from gaming) to allow personalized career specialization and recognition of varied skills rather than linear hierarchical climbing.
*Source:* Suggestion that employees could chart personalized career paths similar to games, developing specialized skills acknowledged by the org.
*Cross-reference:* Also logged as STACK MATERIAL: Gamified Metrics Proposed for Transparent Career Progression Models.

### PROVOCATION

**Does Quantified Meritocracy Increase Authority Contestability**
Proposed game-based metric systems for advancement may increase transparency but could simultaneously reduce human discretion, potentially creating a new rigidity that limits genuine contestation.
*Source:* Tension arises from proposed reliance on quantifiable game metrics replacing human judgment in promotion and value assessment contexts.
*Cross-reference:* None.
