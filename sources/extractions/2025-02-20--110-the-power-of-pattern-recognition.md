### PATTERN CANDIDATE

**Cognitive Diversity Driven toward Decentralized Commons by Institutional Rigidity**
*Phenomenon:* High-value pattern recognition skills held by neurodivergent individuals are systematically filtered out by standardized corporate hiring and sensory environments.
*Context:* AI development, enterprise technology sectors.
*Forces:* Neurotypical social norms in hiring, sensory-unfriendly infrastructure, and rigid organizational hierarchies vs. the cognitive demands of AI ethics and data annotation.
*Consequences:* Talent migration to open-source and startup ecosystems where meritocracy overrides social signaling, potentially fragmenting governance authority away from centralized institutions.
