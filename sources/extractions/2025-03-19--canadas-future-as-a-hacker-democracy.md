**Title:** Digitally Owned Infrastructure Ensures Information Sovereignty

**Layer:** 3 (Legibility & Metrics)

**Content:** Physical and digital infrastructure must be publicly owned or encrypted-by-default to prevent external surveillance and data harvesting. Systems requiring visibility to outsiders must be shielded from external legibility demands.

**Title:** Decentralized Governance Reduces Centralized Coercion Points

**Layer:** 4 (Decision Systems & Authority)

**Content:** Shifting decision power to municipalities and local networks removes single points of failure and reduces effectiveness of federal coercion. Authority is distributed to maximize resilience and contestability.

**Source:** Argument for "radically decentralized" governance, localized autonomy, and peer-to-peer legal systems.

**Cross-reference:** Also logged as CONCEPT: Hacker Democracy.

**Title:** Learning Systems Prioritize Adaptation Over Knowledge Storage

**Layer:** 6 (Human Capacity & Care)
