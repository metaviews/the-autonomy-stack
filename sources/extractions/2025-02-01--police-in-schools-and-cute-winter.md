**Content:** When local governance bodies implement safety protocols (like restorative justice) that conflict with state security standards, central authorities utilize legislative power to dissolve the body. ## CONCEPT
### Algospeak Mechanism for Censorship Evasion
**Definition:** A method of substituting politically sensitive or flagged terminology with benign, everyday language to evade content moderation systems. It functions as a counter-architecture to algorithmic censorship, prioritizing community legibility over platform compliance.
**Source:** Description of TikTok trend repurposing fashion terms for political discourse.
**Cross-Note:** Also logged as STACK MATERIAL (Layer 3).

### Restorative Justice as Governance Infrastructure
**Definition:** A system of harm reduction and reconciliation replacing punitive measures within public institutions. Research indicates it reduces aggression and improves academic outcomes, serving as an alternative authority model within educational spaces that challenges traditional policing paradigms.
**Source:** Newsletter summary of research on school discipline methods.
**Connection:** Challenges the definition of Safety in Layer 4 Authority and Layer 6 Care.

## PROVOCATION

**Content:** The text posits restorative justice as safer than policing, while the State posits policing as safer.
