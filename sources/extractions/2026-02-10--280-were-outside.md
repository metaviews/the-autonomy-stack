### PATTERN CANDIDATE
**The Stillness Trap in Institutional Design**

**Content:**
**Phenomenon:** Institutions prioritize fixed physical arrangements (chairs, rooms) to maintain perceived control and simulate stability.
**Context:** Modern legislatures, corporate boards, expert panels.
**Forces:** Desire to manage volatility, protect status quo, professionalize authority through "seated" legitimacy.
**Consequences:** Reduced adaptability in face of ecological/social volatility; exclusion of disabled or non-standard bodies; disconnect between decision-makers and lived reality.

**Source Note:**
Derived from analysis of "Power has a preferred posture" and "Sitting politics presumes a world of constants."

**Cross-reference:**
Also logged as STACK MATERIAL (Layer 4).

### CONCEPT
**Seated Politics: Authority Organized Through Fixed Posture**

**Content:**
A political mode where legitimacy is enacted through fixed physical arrangements (chairs, rooms) to suppress volatility and define participation. It confuses immobility with seriousness and treats agency as conditional upon adapting bodies to furniture.

**Source Note:**
Defined in response to text analysis of "Sedentary living is killing us cognitively and politically."

**Cross-reference:**
Connects to STACK MATERIAL Layer 4 and PROVOCATION regarding Legibility.

### CONCEPT
**Embodied Cognition: Cognition Emerges From Body-Space Negotiation**

**Content:**
Analytical framework positing that thinking is not confined to the brain but emerges from the ongoing negotiation between body, space, and environment. Posture and movement alter attention, memory, and emotional regulation, meaning governance space is political infrastructure.

**Source Note:**
Based on reference to "decades of work in embodied cognition" and "Walking has been shown to significantly increase creative output."

**Cross-reference:**
Connects to PATTERN CANDIDATE regarding cognitive impacts on decision systems.

### PROVOCATION
**Legibility Requires Immobility: A False Premise**

**Content:**
The Stack values "legibility over expertise." This text suggests current legibility (fixed agendas, seated minutes, predictable outcomes) is a manufactured fiction designed to hide systemic volatility. True legibility might require tracking fluid, embodied movement, rendering current Layer 3 (Metrics) metrics obsolete.

**Source Note:**
Derived from the argument that "Sitting politics presumes a world of constants... But the world we inhabit is defined by variables."
