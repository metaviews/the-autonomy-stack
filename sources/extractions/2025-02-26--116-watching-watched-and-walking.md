### CONCEPT

**Definition of Five Eyes Intelligence Framework**
- **Synthesized Content:** A formal intelligence sharing alliance among five English-speaking nations. Governance structure relies on consensus and classified data pooling, functioning as a supranational security layer that assumes stability but remains vulnerable to bilateral political coercion.
- **Source Note:** Newsletter defines Five Eyes as an alliance facing expulsion rumors due to policy disagreements.
- **Cross-reference:** None.

**Definition of Proliferated Constellation Architecture**
- **Synthesized Content:** A satellite system design utilizing mass deployment of hundreds of individual nodes rather than few specialized assets. This approach prioritizes redundancy and coverage, shifting the governance challenge from securing single targets to regulating a diffuse network of commercial and military infrastructure.
- **Source Note:** NRO using SpaceX's mass deployment model (Starshield) for spy satellites instead of traditional Keyholes.
- **Cross-reference:** Connects to STACK MATERIAL regarding Layer 4/5 shifts.
