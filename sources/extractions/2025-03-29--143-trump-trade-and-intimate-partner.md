### SIGNAL
**Framing Geopolitics Through Intimate Violence Dynamics**

International trade disputes are increasingly being recognized not as rational economic strategy but as psychological coercion. This reframing makes visible power dynamics where material interdependence is weaponized to punish autonomy-seeking behaviors, shifting the necessary response from diplomatic negotiation to boundary enforcement and safety planning for dependent entities. The approach highlights hidden emotional drivers in statecraft previously categorized solely as national interest.

### PATTERN CANDIDATE
**Sovereignty Penalty Loop in Hegemonic Relations**

A recurring dynamic where a dominant authority utilizes economic or defense dependencies to sanction partners asserting independence. The phenomenon emerges in asymmetric relationships where hierarchy is normalized. Forces producing it include a hegemon’s fear of losing centrality and the lack of alternative support systems. Consequences of ignoring it are normalization of abuse and escalated coercion; engaging requires participants to build independent material baselines and alternative institutions to secure viable exit options.
