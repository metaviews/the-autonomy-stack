# 188: Harvesting Lies

**Disinformation, Food Security, and the Battle for Agricultural Truth in a Fractured Canada**  
*2025-05-27*

---

### SIGNAL

**Disinformation Strategy Targets Food Security Systems Now**
Disinformation has shifted from side-effect to a deliberate strategy to stall environmental regulation by targeting agriculture and food security.
Source note: Text identifies disinformation as a tactic used by agri-business and ideological actors to manufacture doubt specifically in farming sectors.

**Rural Communities Reliance on Facebook as Infrastructure**
Rural residents treat Facebook groups as essential infrastructure for safety and logistics, replacing local journalism.
Source note: Newsletter notes Facebook is how rural people learn about road conditions, repairs, and weather, creating a dependency on algorithmic curation.
*Also logged as STACK MATERIAL: Material Base Layer Infrastructure*

### PATTERN CANDIDATE

**Corporate Doubt Strategy to Stall Environmental Regulation**
This pattern involves manufacturing doubt to stop debate rather than win it, specifically targeting climate and agricultural science to protect corporate interests. It emerges when regulatory frameworks threaten profit models, resulting in destabilized trust and policy paralysis.
Source note: The text compares this to the "Big Tobacco and Big Oil" playbook used to stall environmental regulation.
*Also logged as STACK MATERIAL: Decision Systems & Authority*

**Elite Dissenter Archetype Blends Populism with Insider Access**
Political figures like RFK Jr. synthesize anti-authority populism with access to elite power structures, sanctifying disinformation while claiming outsider authenticity. This creates a trust vacuum where traditional expertise is delegitimized.
Source note: Newsletter describes RFK Jr. as representing a dangerous synthesis of anti-authority sentiment and elite influence in health and agriculture.

### STACK MATERIAL

**Layer 1 & 3: Tech Platforms Function as Civic Infrastructure**
Current framing often views social platforms as marketing tools; this case requires treating them as essential utility infrastructure (like roads) where governance failures (news bans) create material safety risks. This extends the Material Base definition to include digital lifelines.
Source note: Newsletter states Facebook is essential infrastructure for rural safety (roads, septic, snowplow info) which impacts physical operations.
*Also logged as SIGNAL: Rural Communities Reliance on Facebook as Infrastructure*

**Layer 2 & 5: Intermediaries Needed for Institutional Trust**
Trust in institutions (science, government) cannot be repaired solely through data dissemination. It requires rebuilding "multi-disciplinary intermediaries" who bridge gaps between settler, Indigenous, rural, and urban populations.
Source note: Newsletter calls for community-powered public-interest media and multi-disciplinary intermediaries to fix the information breakdown.

**Layer 4 & 6: Trust Requires Solidarity Over Facts**
The solution to disinformation is not "certainty" (expertise) but "community" (solidarity). This challenges the Stack’s principle of "legibility over expertise" by suggesting that legibility data alone cannot fix authority decay without relational repair.
Source note: Newsletter argues antidote to disinformation is community, not fact-checking, to protect food security and democratic life.
*Also logged as PROVOCATION: Fact-Checking Insufficient Without Restoring Community Connection*

### CONCEPT

**Ontologies Define How We Construct Real and Political**
Ontologies are the living, breathing structures that shape how systems classify and connect objects, identities, and politics. They are not neutral but scaffolding that can empower or constrain, requiring examination of who builds them.
Source note: Salon announcement defines ontologies as the hidden taxonomies organizing institutions and identities.

**Semantic Webs and Knowledge Graphs Organize Systems**
The text explicitly references semantic webs of AI and knowledge graphs as parallel structures to political taxonomies. These technical frameworks are presented as active agents in shaping meaning.
Source note: Text includes "ontologies, the semantic webs of AI and knowledge graphs" as parallel structures governing classification.

### PROVOCATION

**Fact-Checking Insufficient Without Restoring Community Connection**
The text directly challenges the common assumption that providing better facts (legibility) fixes authority crises. It posits that disinformation thrives on disconnection, meaning authority is built on solidarity, not information quality.
Source note: Newsletter states "We won’t win this battle with fact-checking alone" and "antidote... is not certainty. It’s community."
