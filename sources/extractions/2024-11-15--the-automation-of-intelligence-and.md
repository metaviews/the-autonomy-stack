### PATTERN CANDIDATE

**Algorithmic Systems Shield Governance From Accountability**

The phenomenon occurs when complex governance decisions are delegated to automated systems specifically to avoid human accountability. The context is high-stakes policy execution where error is inevitable but blame is politically costly. The forces driving this include the demand for administrative efficiency and the complexity of data environments.

Engaging this pattern results in opaque decision-making where failure is un-informative because the "black box" prevents diagnostic analysis. Ignoring it allows the erosion of democratic oversight.

*Source note: Newsletter discusses how decision-makers "bypass traditional channels of accountability" by deferring blame to "the algorithm."*

*ALSO LOGGED AS CONCEPT: Algorithmic Authority*
