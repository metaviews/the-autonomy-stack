### SIGNAL

**Title:** Revival of 1798 Alien Enemies Act for Domestic Control
**Synthesized Content:**
The U.S. government is repurposing an 18th-century wartime statute designed for foreign nationals during war to enforce mass deportations and detain political dissidents during peacetime. This strategy bypasses standard judicial oversight to consolidate executive power and suppress dissent under the guise of national security.
**Source Note:** Describes the administration's reinterpretation of the Alien Enemies Act in the newsletter's opening analysis.

### CONCEPT

**Title:** Concept: Executive Limits in Youngstown Case
**Synthesized Content:**
A legal framework established in *Youngstown Sheet & Tube Co. v. Sawyer* asserting that executive power has limits and cannot exceed congressionally authorized authority, even during crises.
**Source Note:** Cited by ACLU/NILC lawsuits as a constitutional benchmark.
**Cross-Reference:** Core benchmark for contesting Layer 4 Authority; see STACK MATERIAL.

**Title:** Concept: Alien Enemies Act of 1798
**Synthesized Content:**
A federal statute originally designed to allow the U.S. president to detain or deport foreign nationals from hostile nations during times of war, now subject to peacetime reinterpretation.
**Source Note:** Described as the primary tool for current administration's enforcement strategy.
**Cross-Reference:** Historical authority mechanism now destabilizing Layer 4; see SIGNAL.

**Title:** Concept: Vienna Convention on Consular Relations
**Synthesized Content:**
International treaty guaranteeing foreign nationals access to consular assistance when detained abroad, frequently violated during aggressive border enforcement operations.
**Source Note:** Cited regarding Jasmine Mooney's detention and Canadian diplomatic complaints.
**Cross-Reference:** Connects Layer 4 Authority to international Legibility and accountability.
