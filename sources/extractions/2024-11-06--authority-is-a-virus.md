**Title:** Algorithmic Mutation Becomes Resistance Tool
**Content:** Linguistic adaptation—specifically spelling errors and word replacements like "unalive" for "dead"—is emerging as a common tactic to evade platform moderation and surveillance algorithms rather than engaging in direct political discourse.
**Source:** The newsletter identifies "Algo speak" as a new lexicon used by users sidestepping bans to evade algorithmic detection, treating language as a site of evasion.

### PATTERN CANDIDATE

**Title:** Evasion as Validation of Authoritarian Reach
**Content:** When users mutate language or behavior specifically to bypass control systems, they inadvertently confirm the system's dominance and power to define norms. Resistance requires the system to exist, yet the act of hiding validates the system's surveillance capabilities.
**Context:** Appears in digital governance, corporate content moderation, and state surveillance contexts where users adapt to restrictions rather than dismantling them.
**Forces:** Algorithmic monitoring, normalized surveillance, and the framing of control as "protection" or "neutrality."
**Consequences:** Creates a feedback loop where evasion reinforces the authority being evaded, making the "virus" of authority appear more insidious and inescapable.
**Cross-reference:** Also logged as PROVOCATION: The Legibility Trap.
