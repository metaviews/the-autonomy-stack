# 144: Adolescence and the Failure of Grown-Ups

**The System Isn’t Broken—It’s Working as Designed**  
*2025-03-30*

---

### SIGNAL
**Emotional Repression Fuels Algorithmic Extremism Growth**
Society’s cultural norm of prioritizing emotional stoicism and vulnerability suppression creates a vacuum that predatory digital networks exploit to monetize pain. When humans cannot connect sincerely, they turn to algorithms that reward domination and performative cruelty, accelerating radicalization among the isolated. This shift suggests that social cohesion mechanisms are failing to regulate emotional output in the digital marketplace.
*Source Note: Paraphrases newsletter analysis of societal inability to be vulnerable feeding digital machines and the rise of figures like the Tate brothers.*

### PATTERN CANDIDATE
**Forensic Diagnosis Without Remedial Action Capacity**
A recurring social dynamic where institutions possess high legibility regarding their own failures but lack the political will to implement solutions. Groups achieve perfect self-diagnosis of decay while simultaneously maintaining structures that prevent repair. The consequence is a "forensic society" that identifies problems indefinitely without fixing them, leading to gradual institutional erosion and public disengagement from official channels.
*Source Note: Distills the newsletter's observation of a society forensic in self-diagnosis but impotent in response.*
*Cross-reference: Also logged as STACK MATERIAL: Institutional Care Performance Replaces Actual Support (Layer 2, 6).*

### STACK MATERIAL
**Institutional Care Performance Replaces Actual Support**
*Relevant Layers: 2 (Commons & Institutions), 4 (Decision Systems & Authority), 6 (Human Capacity & Care)*
Governance systems often optimize for procedural compliance (paperwork, protocols) rather than genuine care outcomes. When education, justice, and health sectors function as administrative rituals, they create legitimacy without safety. This pattern complicates the Stack’s framing of institutions as inherently protective, suggesting they may be designed to maintain order through performance rather than resolve human needs.
*Source Note: Summarizes the text’s description of systems function more as performance than care, with adults trained to follow protocols rather than connect.*
*Cross-reference: Also logged as PATTERN CANDIDATE: Forensic Diagnosis Without Remedial Action Capacity.*

### CONCEPT
**Authority as Abandonment**
A state of governance where formal structures of authority exist but fail to provide tangible protection or resource distribution. In this condition, the mere presence of power is interpreted by subjects as the absence of care, leading vulnerable populations to seek alternative legitimacy in radical or predatory groups. This concept distinguishes "lack of authority" (anarchy) from "authority as abandonment" (performative hierarchy).
*Source Note: Definition derived from the newsletter’s thesis that "authority today is often indistinguishable from abandonment" and power without compassion leads to radicalization.*

### PROVOCATION
**System Decay May Be Intentional Design Feature**
The claim that institutional failure is not a bug but a feature challenges the Stack’s principle of resilience through improvement. If systems are "working as designed" to produce alienation, then the design objective itself must be questioned rather than merely patched. This suggests that fixing legibility or efficiency is insufficient when the core output of the system is structural abandonment.
*Source Note: Reflects the newsletter’s subtitle and central thesis that the system isn’t broken but working as designed.*
