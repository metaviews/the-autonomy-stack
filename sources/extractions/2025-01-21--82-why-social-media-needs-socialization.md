## SIGNAL
**Platform executives aligning with authoritarian state interests**
Social media leadership is actively coordinating with incoming political regimes, prioritizing profit and access over public interest. This signals a collapse of platform neutrality, where infrastructure owners position themselves to serve state power rather than the user base. This alignment facilitates the consolidation of authority by embedding political influence within the digital infrastructure itself.

*Also logged as PATTERN CANDIDATE: Platform-Regime Complicity*

## PATTERN CANDIDATE
**The stagnation of "social" media despite increased interaction**
Phenomenon: Media systems evolve from one-way broadcast control to two-way interaction without shifting underlying power dynamics. Context: Modern social media platforms facilitate "connection" but remain under private corporate ownership with opaque governance. Forces: Profit maximization (advertising/engagement metrics) creates alignment with outrage rather than community care. Consequence: Without shifting ownership and algorithmic control, these systems remain extractive and politically weaponizable despite superficial social features.
*Source note: Text contrasts historical broadcast media (control) with current social media (interaction but profit-driven) to highlight the gap between "social interface" and "social reality".*
*Also logged as SIGNAL: Platform executives aligning with authoritarian state interests*

## STACK MATERIAL
**Layer 2 (Commons & Institutions): Collective Ownership Models**
Proposal to replace shareholder-owned platforms with user, creator, and community ownership structures. This requires establishing legal frameworks for digital infrastructure as a common asset rather than private capital. Extends existing co-op models into the digital governance layer.
*Source note: Platforms owned collectively by users, creators, and communities rather than corporate shareholders.*
*Also logged as CONCEPT: Socialized Media (Ownership Pillar)*

**Layer 3 (Legibility & Metrics): Data Sovereignty & Algorithmic Agency**
Proposal for individual and community sovereignty over data usage and algorithmic function. Shifts the goal from "transparency" (seeing the code) to "agency" (the ability to modify the code). Legibility must be paired with the capacity to intervene in system logic.
*Source note: Individuals having sovereignty over data... transparent, community-driven algorithms... Not just transparency, but agency.*
*Also logged as CONCEPT: Agency vs Transparency*

**Layer 4 (Decision Systems & Authority): Democratic Platform Governance**
Decision-making processes for media platforms must be democratic and participatory rather than administrative or executive. Authority is transferred from C-suite to the community served. This requires formal voting or representation mechanisms for content moderation and feature prioritization.
*Source note: Decision-making processes that are democratic and participatory, ensuring media serves the public interest.*
*Also logged as PATTERN CANDIDATE: The stagnation of "social" media*

**Layer 5 (Knowledge & Intelligence): Dialogue over Persuasion**
Media ecosystems currently prioritize persuasion and monetization, leading to division and conspiracy. New systems should prioritize dignity and dialogue. This shifts the knowledge metric from engagement metrics to community cohesion and trust.
*Source note: Current system thrives on keeping us addicted, divided... goals of persuasion and monetization overshadow collaboration.*
*Also logged as PROVOCATION: Interaction does not equal socialization*

**Layer 6 (Human Capacity & Care): Dignity as Design Specification**
Technical systems should not dehumanize users. Care, respect, and dignity are functional requirements for the media layer, replacing outrage and dehumanization in the feed design. Human capacity is degraded by "addicted, divided" algorithmic environments.
*Source note: Media ecosystem where dignity, respect, and dialogue replace the dehumanization, disrespect, and conspiracy.*

## CONCEPT
**Socialized Media (Re-Definition)**
Proposed Definition: A media framework where socialization is applied to four vectors: (1) Ownership (collective/user), (2) Control (democratic/participatory), (3) Data (individual/community sovereignty), and (4) Algorithms (community-driven values). Distinct from state ownership; emphasizes user collectives over corporate or government monopolies.
*Source note: Imagine a future where media is Socialized in Ownership, Control, Data, Algorithms.*
*Also logged as STACK MATERIAL: Layer 2; Layer 3; Layer 4*

**Agency vs Transparency**
Proposed Definition: The distinction between knowing how a system works (transparency) and having the power to modify how the system works (agency). A design condition where visibility alone is insufficient without the capacity to alter the outcome.
*Source note: Not just transparency, but agency... A new rhythm to our algorithms... one we can... challenge, and modify.*
*Also logged as STACK MATERIAL: Layer 3*

## PROVOCATION
**"Social media" is a misnomer; current systems are neither broadcast nor social**
Challenges the assumption that "interaction" equals "socialization." If platforms prioritize profit over connection and allow power to be concentrated, they function as a new form of centralized control (broadcast 2.0) rather than a distributed community space. The label "social media" may obscure the reality of the power structure.
*Source note: What if what we call "social media" today isn't actually social media? What if it's media in the process of being socialized?*
*Also logged as PATTERN CANDIDATE: The stagnation of "social" media*
