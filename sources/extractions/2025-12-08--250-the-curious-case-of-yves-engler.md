### STACK MATERIAL

**Layer 4: Processes manage dissent rather than channeling it**
Current decision systems in political organizations prioritize predictability and defensiveness, neutralizing ideological heterodoxy through procedural gatekeeping mechanisms.
Source Note: Newsletter states internal processes are designed to manage dissent rather than channel it, creating a parliamentary shell optimized for predictability.

### CONCEPT

**Moral Exceptionalism assumes activists occupy higher moral ground**
The belief that outsiders possess sharper vision and honesty than insiders presumes virtue rather than earning trust through political connection.
Source Note: Loreto identifies this stance as strategically suffocating for activists seeking to transform institutions from the outside.

### CONCEPT

**Relational Authority requires cohabitation with opposing political positions**
Authority is formed not through moral purity but through the capacity to understand and move those with whom one disagrees.
Source Note: Text describes leadership as demanding "sitting with people whose positions you find inadequate... to understand how to move them."
Cross-reference: None

### CONCEPT

**Zombified Party describes institutions optimized for predictability**
A state where a political organization retains form but loses movement DNA, functioning as a shell optimized for parliamentary predictability.
Source Note: The newsletter warns the NDP risks becoming a "zombified" one dependent on how the leadership race unfolds regarding the party's future.
Cross-reference: See STACK MATERIAL (Layer 2: Commons & Institutions)

### PROVOCATION

**Public performance of authority outweighs substantive private organization**
Visibility and performative fluency (e.g., debates) can dictate leadership outcomes more effectively than long-term organizing capacity or policy depth.
Source Note: Engler missed a bilingual debate opportunity, proving a single performance can matter more than a year of organizing in authority contests.
