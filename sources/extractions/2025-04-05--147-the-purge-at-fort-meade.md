### PATTERN CANDIDATE
**Crisis-Driven Centralization of Intelligence Authority**

This creates a feedback loop where expertise is removed just as the system needs it most. Forces include external agitation by non-state actors and internal consolidation of power. Consequence is reduced operational capacity and increased internal leakage (whistleblowing).
*Source: Haugh dismissal coincides with tariff-induced market crash and protests, while Loomer claims credit for removal.*
