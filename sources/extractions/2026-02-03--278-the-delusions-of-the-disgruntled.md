## SIGNAL
**Consumer Expectations Fuel Conspiracy When State Fails**
Citizens increasingly view state interactions through a customer service lens, creating emotional vulnerability when authority acts structurally rather than responsively. This cognitive dissonance transforms structural distance into perceived betrayal, priming populations to accept conspiratorial narratives that restore a sense of agency against impersonal systems.

*Source note: Newsletter argues dissatisfaction stems from "violated expectations" of personal service when interacting with public institutions.*

## PATTERN CANDIDATE
**Personalizing Structural Problems Breeds Conspiratorial Authority**
*Phenomenon:* Citizens attribute complex structural failures (droughts, shocks) to individualized accounting errors or moral failings.
*Context:* Visible in high-pressure regions (Canadian prairies) where climate and market volatilities meet political grievance.
*Forces:* The cultural fiction of government as employer combined with economic precarity.
*Consequence:* Engagement shifts from policy reform to "hybrid civil war," where legitimacy is contested rather than managed; conspiracy fills the void where authority offers procedure rather than answers.

*Source note: Describes the collision of "comforting inversion of power" with "structural" reality in rural communities.*

*Definition:* A state of political conflict where disputes transcend specific policy demands to contest the fundamental model of authority itself (service vs. structure), utilizing estrangement and identity hardening.
*Connection:* Extends "Decision Systems & Authority" beyond traditional partisan dispute into systemic identity conflict.

*Definition:* Revenue collection functionally acts as a regulatory tool to discipline markets and legitimize extraction, distinct from a pooled consumer account for service purchase.
*Connection:* Reframes "Material Base" (funding) into a mechanism of "Decision Systems" (legitimacy/control).
