### SIGNAL
**Shift To Algorithmic Authority Dominance Models**
Attention is shifting from purely institutional or human authority to automated systems that govern information flow, positioning algorithms as a primary vector of power. This signal illuminates a changing context where visibility is curated by code rather than solely by structure or expertise. Observations include social media platforms and search engines actively shaping public opinion through data processing.

### PATTERN CANDIDATE
**Hybrid Authority Models Straddle Three Power Domains**
Observation indicates that functional authorities rarely rely on a single source of power. Instead, entities typically combine elements from two of the three CIA domains (Cognitive, Institutional, Algorithmic) to mitigate disadvantages, such as trading institutional legitimacy for algorithmic reach. Ignoring this hybrid dynamic leads to systems that lack necessary stability, reach, or expertise.
