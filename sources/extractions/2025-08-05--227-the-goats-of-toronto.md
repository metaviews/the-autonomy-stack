### SIGNAL
**Neon Vests Mark Normalization of Urban Livestock**

Livestock entering public transit and utility spaces signifies a shift from novelty to institutionalized utility. The visual markers (neon vests, tablets, checklists) transform animals into visible, legible components of municipal logistics rather than unregulated nature. This integration signals that the city’s operational baseline is expanding beyond asphalt and machinery to include biological actors as standard infrastructure components.

**Source Note:** Narrative description of a goat on the subway wearing a neon vest and being led through a transit station with a tablet checklist.

### PATTERN CANDIDATE
**Bio-Infrastructure Replacing Industrial Municipal Service Systems**

*Phenomenon:* Living organisms deployed to perform municipal maintenance tasks previously handled by industrial crews.
*Context:* Urban areas facing high maintenance costs, waste volume crises, and declining community engagement.
*Forces:* Economic necessity, ecological needs, and desire for mental health benefits derived from nature contact.
*Consequences:* High resilience and community ownership if governance adapts; legal friction and ecological risk if containment fails.

**Source Note:** Manifesto claims goats reduce organic waste by 32%, clear invasive plants cheaper than crews, and strengthen neighborhood networks compared to failing existing systems.

**Cross-Reference:** Also logged as STACK MATERIAL: Material Base; STACK MATERIAL: Commons & Institutions.
