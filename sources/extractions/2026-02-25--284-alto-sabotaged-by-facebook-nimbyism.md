### PATTERN CANDIDATE
**The Divergence of Timelines accelerates distrust in large projects**
The phenomenon where bureaucratic procedures (slow, linear, technical timelines) clash with social media dynamics (fast, emotional, fragmented timelines).
*Context:* Large-scale infrastructure projects (e.g., Alto rail, LRT builds) in environments with high institutional fatigue.
*Forces:* Social media compresses distance between individual anxiety and collective organization; partial information in planning phases allows imagination to fill gaps with worst-case interpretations.
*Consequences:* Projects transition from technical disputes to identity defense; formal legal authority remains intact but social license erodes, increasing political cost and reputational risk.
*Also logged as SIGNAL: Authority shifts from procedural compliance to real-time narrative contestation.*

### CONCEPT

A metric for legitimacy where public acceptance depends on the consistency of a project's story and intent, not just its adherence to legal rules.
*Definition:* The alignment between the official rationale for authority and the perceived reality of stakeholder impact.
*Connection:* Challenges the Stack's emphasis on "legibility over expertise" by adding "narrative" as a third variable in decision-making.
*Source Note:* Officials believe authority is anchored in procedure, yet public evaluation judges narrative coherence and whether "what aren’t they telling us."
