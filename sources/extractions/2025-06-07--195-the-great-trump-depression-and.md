### PATTERN CANDIDATE
**Authority Decoupling from Economic Medium**
Phenomenon: When the medium used to enforce authority (e.g., currency, ledgers) loses legibility or trust, the underlying authority relations must shift. Context: High-inflation or financial crisis environments where the medium fails to coordinate material needs. Forces: Institutional corruption and speculative decoupling from production increase fragility. Consequences: If ignored, the system faces total default; if engaged, it forces a transition to trust-based or planning-based coordination.
Source Note: Describes the 2020s/2030s crash where banks failed and states defaulted, leading to the rise of mutual aid.
Cross-Reference: Also logged as STACK MATERIAL: Planning Replaces Pricing for Resource Allocation.
