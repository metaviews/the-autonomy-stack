Closing Set: Dancing Toward the Exit

At this “music festival,” there’s no merch tent — the souvenirs are court victories, FOIA disclosures, and released detainees. The joke in the code is that joy and defiance can live side-by-side; that the revolution doesn’t just march, it moves to a beat.

If Goldman’s dancing revolutionaries were alive in 2025, they’d be livestreaming from the barricades under the hashtag “music festival” — not to hide, but to remind us that even under surveillance, we can choose our own rhythm.
