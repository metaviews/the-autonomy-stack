# Convivial Tools

## Working Definition

Convivial tools are tools that expand practical human agency without monopolizing knowledge, deskilling users, or making ordinary life dependent on expert-controlled systems.

## Associated Thinkers

Ivan Illich is the central seed thinker. Bookchin helps connect tool autonomy to ecological and institutional forms.

## Source Claims

- Open Library describes Illich's *Tools for Conviviality* as addressing technology, institutionalized expertise, technocratic elites, and the reconquest of practical knowledge.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Conviviality is not friendliness or ease of use. A tool is autonomy-preserving when users can understand, repair, adapt, and refuse it without losing basic agency.

## Stack Application

Convivial tools are crucial to agentic governance: agents should extend judgment and capacity without creating radical dependency.

## Tensions and Cautions

Powerful tools often become infrastructure. Once they become required for participation, they may cease to be convivial.
