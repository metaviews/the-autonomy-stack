# Public Freedom

## Working Definition

Public freedom is freedom enacted with others in shared space through speech, action, appearance, and participation in a common world.

## Associated Thinkers

Hannah Arendt is the central seed thinker. Goldman adds an anti-authoritarian and embodied dimension to public freedom under repression.

## Source Claims

- The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entry on Arendt emphasizes action, freedom, natality, and plurality.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Public freedom challenges autonomy as private possession. Agency becomes real where words and actions can appear, be judged, and matter among others.

## Stack Application

Public freedom maps strongly to Layer 4 decision systems and Layer 6 relational capacity.

## Tensions and Cautions

Public freedom can exclude those whose labor, care obligations, disability, legal status, or repression prevent appearance in public space.
