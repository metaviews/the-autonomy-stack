# Free Association

## Working Definition

Free association is voluntary coordination among people or groups without centralized command, sustained through federation, mutual obligation, and the ability to exit or revise arrangements.

## Associated Thinkers

Proudhon, Bakunin, Malatesta, and Ward develop different anarchist accounts of association, federation, organization, and everyday self-management.

## Source Claims

- Project Gutenberg records preserve public-domain works by Proudhon and Bakunin.
- Freedom Press presents Ward's *Anarchy in Action* as a practical account of anarchist organization in everyday life.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Association is not the absence of organization. It is organization whose authority remains answerable to the participants it coordinates.

## Stack Application

Free association is relevant to federated governance, agent-to-agent collaboration, contribution protocols, and exit rights from shared systems.

## Tensions and Cautions

Association can reproduce informal domination if accountability, access, and conflict handling are left implicit.
