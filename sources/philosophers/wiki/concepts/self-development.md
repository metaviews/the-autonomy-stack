# Self-Development

## Working Definition

Self-development is the growth of judgment, character, capacity, and individuality under conditions that permit experimentation and resist authoritarian conformity.

## Associated Thinkers

Mill emphasizes individuality, experiments in living, and limits on social coercion. Fromm analyzes why people may flee freedom into authoritarian dependence.

## Source Claims

- Project Gutenberg preserves Mill's *On Liberty* as a public-domain text.
- Routledge presents Fromm's *The Fear of Freedom* as an account of modern freedom and authoritarian escape.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Autonomy is not only a right to be left alone. It is a developmental condition shaped by culture, institutions, fear, and social pressure.

## Stack Application

Self-development is relevant to learning systems, recommendation systems, agent delegation, and governance tools that should expand judgment rather than produce dependency.

## Tensions and Cautions

Self-development can be individualized in ways that ignore material constraints, social coercion, and collective conditions.
