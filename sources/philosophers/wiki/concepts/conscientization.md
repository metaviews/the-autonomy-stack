# Conscientization

## Working Definition

Conscientization is the development of critical awareness through which people read their conditions, name domination, and act collectively to transform them.

## Associated Thinkers

Paulo Freire is the seed thinker for this concept.

## Source Claims

- Bloomsbury presents *Pedagogy of the Oppressed* as a major work on education, oppression, and liberation.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Autonomy requires more than access to information. It requires the capacity to interpret one's situation, contest inherited categories, and participate in transforming shared conditions.

## Stack Application

Conscientization is relevant to agent-facing documentation, deliberation tools, governance literacy, and interfaces that help users and agents see power relations rather than merely execute tasks.

## Tensions and Cautions

Education can become domination when it treats people as passive recipients. Critical formation must preserve agency and avoid coercive liberation.
