# Care vs. Charity

## Tension

Care can build shared capacity and solidarity, while charity can preserve hierarchy by turning need into dependency and gratitude.

## Thinkers Who Illuminate It

Dean Spade, Ruth Wilson Gilmore, Silvia Federici, Peter Kropotkin.

## Source Claims

Verso presents Spade's *Mutual Aid* as focused on solidarity, shared resources, and collective care rather than charity.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Autonomy-preserving care lets people participate in shaping the support they depend on. Charity often leaves power relations intact.

## Stack Application

Use this tension when designing support systems, moderation responses, crisis protocols, or agent assistance flows.

## Open Questions

How can care infrastructure remain accountable to those receiving and providing care?
