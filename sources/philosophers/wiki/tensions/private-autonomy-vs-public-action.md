# Private Autonomy vs. Public Action

## Tension

Autonomy can be understood as private self-direction, but political agency often requires public appearance, speech, action, and participation with others.

## Thinkers Who Illuminate It

Hannah Arendt, Emma Goldman.

## Source Claims

The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entry on Arendt emphasizes action, freedom, natality, and plurality.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Private autonomy protects space for self-direction, but public action is where shared worlds are made and contested. Neither can fully substitute for the other.

## Stack Application

The Stack must preserve both private agency and public contestability.

## Open Questions

How can people excluded from public space still exercise political agency?
