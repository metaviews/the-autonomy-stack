# Moral Autonomy vs. Social Conditions

## Tension

Moral autonomy emphasizes self-legislation and rational agency, but actual self-governance depends on material, relational, educational, and institutional conditions.

## Thinkers Who Illuminate It

Immanuel Kant, Cornelius Castoriadis, Ivan Illich.

## Source Claims

The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy describes Kant's moral philosophy as grounded in autonomy and the rational will.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Kantian autonomy gives a powerful account of moral authorship. The Stack adds that moral agency must be materially and socially sustained.

## Stack Application

Layer 6 and Layer 1 prevent autonomy from becoming purely formal.

## Open Questions

What conditions must exist before appeals to individual responsibility are legitimate?
