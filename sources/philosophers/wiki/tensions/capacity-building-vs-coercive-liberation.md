# Capacity-Building vs. Coercive Liberation

## Tension

Autonomy requires capacities, but authorities can use the language of capacity, self-mastery, or liberation to coerce people in the name of their "true" freedom.

## Thinkers Who Illuminate It

Isaiah Berlin, Immanuel Kant, Michel Foucault.

## Source Claims

Oxford Academic describes Berlin's *Two Concepts of Liberty* as warning about the perversion of positive liberty into totalitarian claims.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Positive liberty is necessary but dangerous. The governance question is how to build capacity without authorizing coercion by those who claim superior knowledge of another's freedom.

## Stack Application

This tension applies to care systems, education, agentic recommendations, and expert governance.

## Open Questions

What contestability mechanisms prevent capacity-building from becoming coercive?
