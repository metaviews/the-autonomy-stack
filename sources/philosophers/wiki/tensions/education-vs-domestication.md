# Education vs. Domestication

## Tension

Education can build critical agency, but it can also train people to adapt to domination.

## Thinkers Who Illuminate It

Paulo Freire, Ivan Illich, bell hooks as a future-wave candidate.

## Source Claims

Bloomsbury presents Freire's *Pedagogy of the Oppressed* as a major work on education and liberation.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Autonomy-oriented education helps people interpret and transform their conditions. Domestication asks them to internalize the categories of the system that governs them.

## Stack Application

This tension applies to documentation, onboarding, agent instruction, user training, and governance literacy.

## Open Questions

When does guidance become conditioning?
