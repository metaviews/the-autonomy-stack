# Bureaucracy vs. Autonomy

## Tension

Bureaucracy can coordinate complex systems, but it can also convert judgment, obligation, and social life into administrative procedure that people cannot meaningfully contest.

## Thinkers Who Illuminate It

David Graeber, James C. Scott, Ivan Illich.

## Source Claims

The Graeber source record frames *Debt* around obligation, law, religion, and political dispute. Scott's source record frames *Seeing Like a State* around top-down planning.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Bureaucracy constrains autonomy when it replaces situated judgment with procedural compliance and makes the procedure itself the authority.

## Stack Application

This tension links directly to Proceduralism as Shield and Procedural Entombment.

## Open Questions

What forms of administration can support coordination without absorbing contestation?
