# Recognition vs. Resurgence

## Tension

Recognition by dominant institutions can offer visibility or rights, but it can also preserve domination by making autonomy depend on approval from the system being contested.

## Thinkers Who Illuminate It

Glen Coulthard, Audra Simpson, Leanne Betasamosake Simpson.

## Source Claims

Duke University Press and University of Minnesota Press present these works as addressing sovereignty, recognition, refusal, land, and Indigenous resurgence.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Resurgence shifts the center of gravity from being recognized by power to rebuilding practices, relations, and jurisdiction on one's own terms.

## Stack Application

This tension applies to identity systems, community consent, data governance, corpus inclusion, and agent legibility.

## Open Questions

When does inclusion become capture?
