# Freedom vs. Security

## Tension

People and institutions may trade autonomy for safety, certainty, belonging, or relief from responsibility.

## Thinkers Who Illuminate It

Erich Fromm, John Stuart Mill, Hannah Arendt.

## Source Claims

Routledge presents Fromm's *The Fear of Freedom* as an analysis of modern freedom and authoritarian escape.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Security can be autonomy-preserving when it reduces precarity. It becomes heteronomous when it demands submission, conformity, or dependency as the price of safety.

## Stack Application

This tension is central to agentic governance, especially safety controls, delegation boundaries, trust defaults, and human override.

## Open Questions

What forms of safety increase agency rather than replacing it?
