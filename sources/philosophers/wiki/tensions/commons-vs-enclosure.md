# Commons vs. Enclosure

## Tension

Commons depend on shared stewardship, but markets, states, platforms, and expert systems can enclose shared resources by converting them into controlled assets.

## Thinkers Who Illuminate It

Elinor Ostrom, Silvia Federici, Peter Kropotkin, David Graeber.

## Source Claims

Cambridge presents Ostrom's *Governing the Commons* as an institutional study of common-pool resource governance.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Enclosure is not only privatization. It can also occur through technical dependency, data capture, administrative control, or loss of local rule-making power.

## Stack Application

This tension matters for open source corpora, agent-readable knowledge bases, protocol governance, and shared operational tooling.

## Open Questions

What rules preserve open use while preventing extraction, depletion, or capture?
