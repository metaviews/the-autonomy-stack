# Tool Use vs. Tool Dependence

## Tension

Tools can expand agency, but they can also create dependence when people lose the knowledge, access, or capacity to act without them.

## Thinkers Who Illuminate It

Ivan Illich, Murray Bookchin.

## Source Claims

Open Library describes Illich's *Tools for Conviviality* as addressing institutionalized expertise, technocratic elites, and practical knowledge.

## Interpretive Synthesis

The autonomy question is not whether tools are powerful, but whether their power remains user-governable, inspectable, repairable, and refusible.

## Stack Application

This tension is central to agentic governance. Agentic tools can either increase human capacity or create a new dependency on opaque intermediaries.

## Open Questions

What makes an agentic tool convivial rather than dependency-producing?
