# Organization vs. Domination

## Tension

Autonomous groups need organization, but organization can harden into hierarchy, command, and informal domination.

## Thinkers Who Illuminate It

Errico Malatesta, Mikhail Bakunin, Pierre-Joseph Proudhon, Colin Ward, Murray Bookchin.

## Source Claims

The Wave 2 source records preserve public-domain and publisher records for anarchist accounts of association, organization, and anti-statism.

## Interpretive Synthesis

The question is not structure versus no structure. The question is whether structure remains revisable, accountable, participatory, and limited by the autonomy of those it coordinates.

## Stack Application

Use this tension for federated protocols, contributor governance, agent collaboration, escalation paths, and authority cards.

## Open Questions

Which forms of coordination make domination harder rather than easier?
