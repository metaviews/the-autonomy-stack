# Cooperation vs. Coercion

## Tension

Collective life requires coordination, but coordination can be produced through voluntary cooperation or through coercive authority.

## Thinkers Who Illuminate It

Peter Kropotkin, Emma Goldman, Murray Bookchin.

## Source Claims

The Project Gutenberg record for Kropotkin's *Mutual Aid* describes the work as emphasizing cooperation as a force in nature and human society.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Cooperation is not the absence of structure. It requires norms, practices, and institutions that sustain action without converting need into domination.

## Stack Application

This tension maps to care, commons, distributed resistance, and authority design.

## Open Questions

What kinds of structure preserve cooperation without hardening into coercion?
