# Simone Weil

## Core Relevance to Autonomy

Weil brings obligation, attention, hunger, rootedness, and affliction into the autonomy corpus.

## Key Works and Source Records

- [Wave 2 source records](../../raw/wave-2-source-records.md)
- *The Need for Roots*

## Source Claims

- Routledge describes *The Need for Roots* as reflecting on religious and political social structures, obligations, hunger, community, rights, and rootlessness.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Weil helps the corpus resist autonomy as pure rights-claim. Agency depends on obligations, attention, and social rootedness.

## Stack Application

- **L6 Human Capacity & Care:** Attention and obligation are care capacities.
- **L1 Material Base:** Hunger and material need directly constrain agency.
- **L4 Decision Systems & Authority:** Rights without obligations may fail as governance.

## Tensions and Cautions

Weil's thought includes authoritarian and ascetic dangers; her work should be used with care rather than canonized.
