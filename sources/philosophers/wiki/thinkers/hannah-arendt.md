# Hannah Arendt

## Core Relevance to Autonomy

Arendt clarifies autonomy as public action among plural others. Freedom is not only private choice; it appears through speech, action, beginning, and participation in a shared world.

## Key Works and Source Records

- [Wave 1 source records](../../raw/wave-1-source-records.md)
- *The Human Condition*

## Source Claims

- The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entry emphasizes action, freedom, natality, and plurality as central to Arendt's political thought.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Arendt helps the corpus resist a private or internal model of autonomy. Agency becomes visible in public space, where people act with others and disclose who they are through words and deeds.

## Stack Application

- **L4 Decision Systems & Authority:** Political agency requires spaces where action can matter.
- **L5 Knowledge & Intelligence:** Shared reality depends on a common world in which perspectives can appear.
- **L6 Human Capacity & Care:** Plurality and worldliness are relational conditions of agency.

## Tensions and Cautions

- Arendt's distinction between public and private life needs careful handling around care, labor, and exclusion.
- Public action can be romanticized if material access to public space is ignored.
