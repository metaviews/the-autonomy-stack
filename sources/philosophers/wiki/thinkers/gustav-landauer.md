# Gustav Landauer

## Core Relevance to Autonomy

Landauer is important for autonomy because he treats social transformation as the creation of new relations, not only seizure or abolition of state machinery.

## Key Works and Source Records

- [Wave 1 source records](../../raw/wave-1-source-records.md)
- *Revolution and Other Writings*

## Source Claims

- PM Press describes *Revolution and Other Writings* as the first comprehensive English collection of Landauer's writings, including *Revolution*, essays, articles, and correspondence.
- PM Press records Landauer as a key figure in German anarchism and notes his role in the Bavarian Council Republic before his murder by reactionary soldiers.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Landauer helps frame the state as reproduced through social relations, not only imposed from above. Autonomy therefore requires building different forms of association in the present.

## Stack Application

- **L2 Commons & Institutions:** Institutions are enacted relations; alternative institutions must be practiced, not only declared.
- **L4 Decision Systems & Authority:** Authority persists when people reproduce state-like relations in everyday governance.
- **L6 Human Capacity & Care:** Community formation is an autonomy condition.

## Tensions and Cautions

- Landauer's spiritual and communal language needs careful interpretation to avoid vagueness.
- His critique of state power should be distinguished from withdrawal from collective responsibility.
