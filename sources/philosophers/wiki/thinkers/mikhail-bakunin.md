# Mikhail Bakunin

## Core Relevance to Autonomy

Bakunin is central to anarchist critiques of state and divine authority, especially where freedom is understood as collective emancipation from domination.

## Key Works and Source Records

- [Wave 2 source records](../../raw/wave-2-source-records.md)
- *God and the State*

## Source Claims

- Project Gutenberg describes *God and the State* as a critique of divine and state authority and a defense of human freedom against oppressive structures.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Bakunin foregrounds the danger that authority justified as higher truth can become enslavement.

## Stack Application

- **L4 Decision Systems & Authority:** Authority must be contestable, especially when it claims transcendent legitimacy.
- **L2 Commons & Institutions:** Anti-statism raises the question of coordination without state command.
- **L6 Human Capacity & Care:** Freedom is social, not merely private.

## Tensions and Cautions

Bakunin's revolutionary anti-authoritarianism needs to be read alongside questions of durable institution-building.
