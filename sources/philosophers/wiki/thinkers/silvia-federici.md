# Silvia Federici

## Core Relevance to Autonomy

Federici connects autonomy to reproductive labor, enclosure, commons, bodies, witch-hunts, capitalism, and feminist struggle.

## Key Works and Source Records

- [Wave 2 source records](../../raw/wave-2-source-records.md)
- *Caliban and the Witch*
- *Re-enchanting the World*

## Source Claims

- PM Press describes *Caliban and the Witch* as a history of the body in the transition to capitalism, investigating witch-hunts, social reproduction, labor power, and self-ownership.
- PM Press describes *Re-enchanting the World* as a feminist critique of the politics of the commons, enclosure, reproductive work, and autonomous spaces against capitalist organization of life.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Federici helps the corpus treat autonomy as impossible without attention to reproduction, bodies, and commons.

## Stack Application

- **L1 Material Base:** Reproductive labor is material infrastructure.
- **L2 Commons & Institutions:** Commons are social relations and sites of struggle.
- **L6 Human Capacity & Care:** Care and reproduction are autonomy conditions.

## Tensions and Cautions

Commons should not be romanticized as safe spaces outside exploitation; Federici frames them as contested terrains.
