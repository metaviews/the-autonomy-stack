# Ruth Wilson Gilmore

## Core Relevance to Autonomy

Gilmore is essential for autonomy under carceral political economy: how state capacity, surplus, racism, and abandonment organize unfreedom.

## Key Works and Source Records

- [Wave 2 source records](../../raw/wave-2-source-records.md)
- *Golden Gulag*

## Source Claims

- University of California Press describes *Golden Gulag* as explaining California prison expansion through political and economic forces, surplus finance capital, labor, land, and state capacity.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Gilmore helps the corpus treat carcerality as material governance, not only legal punishment.

## Stack Application

- **L1 Material Base:** Land, labor, capital, and infrastructure shape agency.
- **L2 Commons & Institutions:** Abolition requires life-affirming institutions.
- **L4 Decision Systems & Authority:** Carceral authority forecloses contestability through confinement.

## Tensions and Cautions

Abolition should not be reduced to institutional removal; it requires alternative capacities for life and care.
