# Voltairine de Cleyre

## Core Relevance to Autonomy

De Cleyre brings feminist and anti-authoritarian ethics into the corpus, especially around freedom, social repression, and the critique of imposed authority.

## Key Works and Source Records

- [Wave 2 source records](../../raw/wave-2-source-records.md)
- *Selected Works of Voltairine de Cleyre*

## Source Claims

- Project Gutenberg describes the selected works as including prose and poetry on social justice, feminism, anarchist principles, individual liberty, and critique of social norms.

## Interpretive Synthesis

De Cleyre helps prevent anarchist autonomy from becoming masculinist, abstract, or detached from lived forms of social constraint.

## Stack Application

- **L4 Decision Systems & Authority:** Authority operates through law and social convention.
- **L6 Human Capacity & Care:** Freedom is shaped by gendered and relational conditions.
- **L3 Legibility & Metrics:** Respectability and norm enforcement define who is recognized as legitimate.

## Tensions and Cautions

Later pages should distinguish de Cleyre's different anarchist phases and avoid flattening her pluralism.
