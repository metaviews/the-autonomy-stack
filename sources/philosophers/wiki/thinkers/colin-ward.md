# Colin Ward

## Core Relevance to Autonomy

Ward is important because he treats anarchism as already-existing practice in everyday life, not only as revolutionary doctrine.

## Key Works and Source Records

- [Wave 2 source records](../../raw/wave-2-source-records.md)
- *Anarchy in Action*

## Source Claims

- Freedom Press describes *Anarchy in Action* as focusing on everyday life: education, housing, organizing, work, play, and social welfare.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Ward helps the corpus see autonomy in ordinary self-organization beneath formal institutions.

## Stack Application

- **L2 Commons & Institutions:** Informal institutions matter.
- **L6 Human Capacity & Care:** Everyday cooperation sustains agency.
- **L4 Decision Systems & Authority:** Non-state coordination can be practical and ordinary.

## Tensions and Cautions

Everyday autonomy can be invisible and fragile when formal power withdraws or represses support.
