# Errico Malatesta

## Core Relevance to Autonomy

Malatesta is important because he thinks concretely about organization without constituted authority.

## Key Works and Source Records

- [Wave 2 source records](../../raw/wave-2-source-records.md)
- *Anarchy*

## Source Claims

- The Anarchist Library record for *Anarchy* presents Malatesta's account of anarchy as society without constituted government and addresses common misreadings of anarchy as disorder.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Malatesta helps distinguish anti-authoritarian organization from disorganization.

## Stack Application

- **L2 Commons & Institutions:** Organization need not imply state authority.
- **L4 Decision Systems & Authority:** Governance can be structured around free association and contestability.
- **L6 Human Capacity & Care:** Cooperation requires practical capacities and habits.

## Tensions and Cautions

Movement texts should be read in historical context, especially around violence, organization, and revolution.
