# Glen Coulthard

## Core Relevance to Autonomy

Coulthard is crucial for autonomy because he critiques recognition by colonial states as a trap that can reproduce domination.

## Key Works and Source Records

- [Wave 2 source records](../../raw/wave-2-source-records.md)
- *Red Skin, White Masks*

## Source Claims

- University of Minnesota Press describes *Red Skin, White Masks* as challenging recognition as a mode of decolonization and emphasizing self-recognition, Indigenous cultural practices, and active decolonization.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Coulthard helps distinguish autonomy from recognition by the power that dominates.

## Stack Application

- **L2 Commons & Institutions:** Institutions can absorb resistance through recognition.
- **L4 Decision Systems & Authority:** Legitimacy cannot come only from the state acknowledging difference.
- **L1 Material Base:** Land and dispossession are central to autonomy.

## Tensions and Cautions

Recognition is not always meaningless, but it becomes dangerous when it substitutes for land, power, and self-determination.
