# Michel Foucault

## Core Relevance to Autonomy

Foucault complicates autonomy by showing that subjects are formed through power and knowledge. Autonomy cannot assume a pre-social self simply choosing outside power.

## Key Works and Source Records

- [Wave 1 source records](../../raw/wave-1-source-records.md)
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entry on Foucault

## Source Claims

- The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entry describes Foucault's account of power as productive rather than merely repressive, operating through practices, institutions, and forms of knowledge.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Foucault helps identify autonomy as a practice under conditions of formation. The question is not only how to remove constraints, but how subjects, categories, norms, and truths are produced.

## Stack Application

- **L3 Legibility & Metrics:** Classification and knowledge systems produce governable subjects.
- **L4 Decision Systems & Authority:** Power operates through dispersed practices, not only sovereign commands.
- **L5 Knowledge & Intelligence:** Knowledge formation is a governance site.

## Tensions and Cautions

- Foucault should not be used to imply that autonomy is impossible.
- The corpus should distinguish critique of subject formation from fatalism about power.
