# Pierre-Joseph Proudhon

## Core Relevance to Autonomy

Proudhon is important for autonomy because he links property, justice, government, mutualism, and federal social organization.

## Key Works and Source Records

- [Wave 2 source records](../../raw/wave-2-source-records.md)
- *What is Property?*

## Source Claims

- Project Gutenberg records *What is Property?* as an inquiry into property, right, government, justice, equality, and the critique of property.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Proudhon helps identify property as a governance relation, not merely an economic fact.

## Stack Application

- **L1 Material Base:** Property structures access to material agency.
- **L2 Commons & Institutions:** Mutualism and federalism offer non-state organizational forms.
- **L4 Decision Systems & Authority:** Property can operate as private authority.

## Tensions and Cautions

Proudhon's work contains serious exclusions and contradictions that later pages must handle directly.
