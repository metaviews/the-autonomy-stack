# Peter Kropotkin

## Core Relevance to Autonomy

Kropotkin is central to autonomy as mutual support rather than isolated self-direction. His work makes cooperation, reciprocity, and decentralized social capacity part of the autonomy question.

## Key Works and Source Records

- [Wave 1 source records](../../raw/wave-1-source-records.md)
- *Mutual Aid: A Factor of Evolution*

## Source Claims

- The Project Gutenberg record describes *Mutual Aid* as a 1902 collection arguing that cooperation and mutual aid are fundamental forces in nature and human societies.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Kropotkin helps shift autonomy away from possessive individualism. Agency is not only protected by reducing interference; it is built through social relations that let people survive, coordinate, and act without centralized command.

## Stack Application

- **L2 Commons & Institutions:** Mutual aid clarifies how non-state institutions can sustain collective agency.
- **L6 Human Capacity & Care:** Care and cooperation appear as infrastructure, not sentiment.
- **L1 Material Base:** Survival systems are organized through shared provisioning, not only market or state distribution.

## Tensions and Cautions

- Mutual aid should not be romanticized as automatically sufficient under crisis or repression.
- Cooperation requires material capacity and social trust; it is not a substitute for analyzing coercive power.
