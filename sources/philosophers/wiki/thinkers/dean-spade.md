# Dean Spade

## Core Relevance to Autonomy

Spade connects mutual aid, abolition, crisis survival, and movement infrastructure in contemporary conditions.

## Key Works and Source Records

- [Wave 2 source records](../../raw/wave-2-source-records.md)
- *Mutual Aid*

## Source Claims

- Verso describes *Mutual Aid* as a handbook for community-based means to fight crises, build solidarity, share resources, and create collective care.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Spade helps define mutual aid as survival work tied to transformative change, not charity or service provision.

## Stack Application

- **L6 Human Capacity & Care:** Care can be movement infrastructure.
- **L2 Commons & Institutions:** Mutual aid groups are governance forms.
- **L4 Decision Systems & Authority:** Non-carceral support requires different authority relations.

## Tensions and Cautions

Mutual aid can be co-opted into unpaid service work if structural change and accountability disappear.
