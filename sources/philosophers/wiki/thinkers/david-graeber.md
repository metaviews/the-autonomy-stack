# David Graeber

## Core Relevance to Autonomy

Graeber connects autonomy to anthropology, debt, bureaucracy, direct action, and prefigurative politics. His work is useful for understanding how obligation and administration become authority.

## Key Works and Source Records

- [Wave 1 source records](../../raw/wave-1-source-records.md)
- *Debt: The First 5,000 Years*

## Source Claims

- The official Graeber site describes *Debt* as a long history of credit systems, debtors and creditors, and disputes around debt, law, religion, guilt, redemption, and political revolt.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Graeber helps reveal how apparently neutral obligations become governance structures. Debt and bureaucracy constrain autonomy by defining what must be repaid, recorded, obeyed, or made administratively legible.

## Stack Application

- **L2 Commons & Institutions:** Direct action and prefigurative practice show institutions being built through use.
- **L4 Decision Systems & Authority:** Debt creates binding obligations that may operate as authority.
- **L3 Legibility & Metrics:** Bureaucracy converts social life into administrable categories.

## Tensions and Cautions

- Graeber's work spans scholarship and activism; corpus pages should distinguish anthropological claims, political arguments, and movement practice.
- Debt should not become a universal explanation for all authority.
