# Elinor Ostrom

## Core Relevance to Autonomy

Ostrom is essential for collective autonomy because she documents self-organization and self-governance in common-pool resource systems.

## Key Works and Source Records

- [Wave 2 source records](../../raw/wave-2-source-records.md)
- *Governing the Commons*

## Source Claims

- Cambridge University Press describes *Governing the Commons* as examining common-pool resources and showing that neither state nor market solutions are uniformly successful.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Ostrom makes autonomy institutional and empirical: people can design rules for shared resources when governance is locally accountable and adaptive.

## Stack Application

- **L2 Commons & Institutions:** Directly informs commons governance.
- **L1 Material Base:** Shared resources require governance.
- **L4 Decision Systems & Authority:** Rule-making and monitoring can be participatory rather than imposed.

## Tensions and Cautions

Ostrom's design principles should not be reduced to a universal template; context matters.
