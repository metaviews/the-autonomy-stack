# Erich Fromm

## Core Relevance to Autonomy

Fromm is important because he asks why people may flee freedom and seek security in authoritarian structures.

## Key Works and Source Records

- [Wave 2 source records](../../raw/wave-2-source-records.md)
- *The Fear of Freedom* / *Escape from Freedom*

## Source Claims

- Routledge describes Fromm's work as addressing contradictory needs for community and freedom, fascism, authoritarianism, and human character in relation to society.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Fromm complicates autonomy by showing that freedom can produce anxiety, isolation, and longing for submission when social conditions do not support mature agency.

## Stack Application

- **L6 Human Capacity & Care:** Autonomy requires psychological and social capacity.
- **L4 Decision Systems & Authority:** Authoritarianism can be desired, not only imposed.
- **L5 Knowledge & Intelligence:** Social character shapes what freedom feels like and means.

## Tensions and Cautions

Fromm should not be used to psychologize authoritarianism away from material and institutional conditions.
