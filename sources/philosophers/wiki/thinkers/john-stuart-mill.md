# John Stuart Mill

## Core Relevance to Autonomy

Mill is a core thinker for liberal autonomy: individuality, freedom of thought, and limits on social and governmental interference.

## Key Works and Source Records

- [Wave 2 source records](../../raw/wave-2-source-records.md)
- *On Liberty*

## Source Claims

- The Project Gutenberg record for *On Liberty* lists chapters on liberty of thought and discussion, individuality, limits to social authority, and applications.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Mill helps clarify autonomy as self-development under conditions where social pressure can be as constraining as law.

## Stack Application

- **L4 Decision Systems & Authority:** The harm principle helps ask when coercion is justified.
- **L6 Human Capacity & Care:** Individuality is a capacity that social conformity can suppress.
- **L3 Legibility & Metrics:** Majority opinion can become a social metric of legitimacy.

## Tensions and Cautions

Mill should not be treated as sufficient for collective or material autonomy; his frame requires supplementation from care, commons, and anti-domination traditions.
