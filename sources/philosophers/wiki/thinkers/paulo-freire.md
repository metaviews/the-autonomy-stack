# Paulo Freire

## Core Relevance to Autonomy

Freire is central for autonomy as developed critical consciousness through dialogical education and collective liberation.

## Key Works and Source Records

- [Wave 2 source records](../../raw/wave-2-source-records.md)
- *Pedagogy of the Oppressed*

## Source Claims

- Bloomsbury describes *Pedagogy of the Oppressed* as addressing oppression, liberation, the banking model of education, problem-posing education, dialogue, and critical consciousness.

## Interpretive Synthesis

Freire frames autonomy as a mutual process of becoming able to read and transform the world, not as individual self-expression alone.

## Stack Application

- **L5 Knowledge & Intelligence:** Systems literacy is a liberation condition.
- **L6 Human Capacity & Care:** Education builds agency through relation.
- **L4 Decision Systems & Authority:** Pedagogy can reproduce domination or enable contestation.

## Tensions and Cautions

Liberatory education can become vanguardism if learners' own agency is displaced by expert direction.
