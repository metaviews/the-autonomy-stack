# Wave 2 Source Records

This file preserves seed source records for the second wave of the philosopher autonomy corpus. It records starting points for later source ingestion; it is not a substitute for reading the works.

## John Stuart Mill

- **On Liberty** — Project Gutenberg record for Mill's 1859 essay on liberty, individuality, freedom of thought and discussion, social authority, and limits on interference.  
  Source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/34901

## Erich Fromm

- **The Fear of Freedom / Escape from Freedom** — Routledge record for Fromm's work on the contradictory human needs for community and freedom, fascism, authoritarianism, and character in relation to society.  
  Source: https://www.routledge.com/The-Fear-of-Freedom/Fromm/p/book/9780415253888

## Simone Weil

- **The Need for Roots** — Routledge record for Weil's posthumous work on obligations, rootedness, religious and political social structures, and the conditions of individual life after social rupture.  
  Source: https://www.routledge.com/The-Need-for-Roots-Prelude-to-a-Declaration-of-Duties-Towards-Mankind/Weil/p/book/9780415271011

## Paulo Freire

- **Pedagogy of the Oppressed** — Bloomsbury Academic record for Freire's work on oppression, liberation, dialogue, critical consciousness, and education as the practice of freedom.  
  Source: https://www.bloomsbury.com/us/pedagogy-of-the-oppressed-9781501314162/

## Elinor Ostrom

- **Governing the Commons** — Cambridge University Press record for Ostrom's study of common-pool resources, self-organization, self-governance, and institutional diversity beyond state or market solutions.  
  Source: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/governing-the-commons/A8BB63BC4A1433A50A3FB92EDBBB97D5

## Pierre-Joseph Proudhon

- **What is Property?** — Project Gutenberg record for Proudhon's inquiry into property, right, government, justice, and equality.  
  Source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/360

## Mikhail Bakunin

- **God and the State** — Project Gutenberg record for Bakunin's critique of divine and state authority and defense of human freedom against oppressive structures.  
  Source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/36568

## Errico Malatesta

- **Anarchy** — Anarchist Library record for Malatesta's 1891 text on anarchy, government, social organization, and the possibility of order without constituted authority.  
  Source: https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/errico-malatesta-anarchy

## Voltairine de Cleyre

- **Selected Works of Voltairine de Cleyre** — Project Gutenberg record for a collection of de Cleyre's anarchist, feminist, poetic, and prose writings.  
  Source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/43098

## Colin Ward

- **Anarchy in Action** — Freedom Press record for Ward's introduction to anarchism as already-existing everyday practice in education, housing, organizing, work, play, and welfare.  
  Source: https://freedompress.org.uk/product/anarchy-in-action-colin-ward/

## Dean Spade

- **Mutual Aid** — Verso record for Spade's book on mutual aid as community-based survival work, solidarity, collective care, and movement infrastructure.  
  Source: https://www.versobooks.com/books/3713-mutual-aid

## Ruth Wilson Gilmore

- **Golden Gulag** — University of California Press record for Gilmore's analysis of prison expansion, surplus, crisis, opposition, and carceral political economy in California.  
  Source: https://www.ucpress.edu/books/golden-gulag/epub-pdf

## Leanne Betasamosake Simpson

- **As We Have Always Done** — University of Minnesota Press record for Simpson's work on Indigenous freedom, radical resistance, resurgence, refusal, land, bodies, and alternatives to settler colonial logics.  
  Source: https://www.upress.umn.edu/9781517903879/as-we-have-always-done/

## Glen Coulthard

- **Red Skin, White Masks** — University of Minnesota Press record for Coulthard's critique of colonial politics of recognition and argument for Indigenous self-recognition, grounded normativity, and active decolonization.  
  Source: https://www.upress.umn.edu/9781452942438/red-skin-white-masks/

## Audra Simpson

- **Mohawk Interruptus** — Duke University Press record for Simpson's study of Kahnawa:ke Mohawk political sovereignty, refusal, nested sovereignty, jurisdiction, and legitimacy under settler states.  
  Source: https://www.dukeupress.edu/mohawk-interruptus

## Silvia Federici

- **Caliban and the Witch** — PM Press record for Federici's history of the body, witch-hunts, primitive accumulation, social reproduction, labor power, and self-ownership in the transition to capitalism.  
  Source: https://pmpress.org/index.php?l=product_detail&p=1575

- **Re-enchanting the World** — PM Press record for Federici's feminist critique of the politics of the commons, enclosure, reproductive work, and autonomous spaces against capitalist organization of life.  
  Source: https://www.pmpress.org/index.php?l=product_detail&p=1021
