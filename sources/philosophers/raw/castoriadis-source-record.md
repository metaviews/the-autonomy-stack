# Cornelius Castoriadis Source Record

This is a seed source record for the philosopher autonomy corpus. It records starting points for later source ingestion; it is not a substitute for reading the works.

## Primary Works and Publisher Records

- **The Imaginary Institution of Society** — first published in French in 1975; English translation by Kathleen Blamey published by MIT Press. MIT Press describes the work as opening political theory and practice toward "the autonomous and explicit self-institution of society."  
  Source: https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262031349/the-imaginary-institution-of-society/

- **Figures of the Thinkable** — posthumous collection published by Stanford University Press. Stanford's description emphasizes Castoriadis's claim that human subjects and society are not predetermined, and links autonomy to creative activity, democracy, education, and the radical imagination.  
  Source: https://www.sup.org/books/theory-and-philosophy/figures-thinkable

## Secondary Source Records

- Evangelos Papadimitropoulos, **"Socialisme ou Barbarie: From Castoriadis' Project of Individual and Collective Autonomy to the Collaborative Commons"**, tripleC, 2016. The abstract frames Castoriadis's socialism through individual and collective autonomy and relates it to collaborative commons.  
  Source: https://www.triple-c.at/index.php/tripleC/article/view/739

- Evangelos Papadimitropoulos, **"Freedom, autonomy, democracy: Castoriadis and the commons"**, Critique, 2022. The abstract links freedom, autonomy, democracy, and the commons in Castoriadis's thought.  
  Source: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/03017605.2022.2050535
