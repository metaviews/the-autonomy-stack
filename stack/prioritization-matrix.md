# Prioritization Matrix - The Autonomy Stack (2026-05-09)

This matrix maps tensions identified in STATE.md against potential operational impact (low/medium/high) and feasibility for immediate dogfooding (low/medium/high).

| Tension / Signal | Operational Impact | Feasibility | Priority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Recommendations
1. **Focus: Agent-legibility vs. Compliance Theater.** This is the most "dogfoodable." We can test if our protocols actually enable accountability or if they are just "paper-pushing" for the agent.
2. **Focus: Acute vs. Chronic Care.** This requires a case study or small-scale simulation, which is high impact for refining the definitions of the stack.
3. **Low Priority:** Speed vs. Contestability. This feels like a cross-cutting systemic constraint that is better solved by iterating on the toolset (e.g. contestability protocols) than solving outright.
