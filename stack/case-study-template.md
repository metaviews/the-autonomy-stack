# Case Study Format

*A template for applying the Autonomy Stack to a specific situation: a governance failure, a design challenge, a coordination problem, or an example of governance holding under pressure.*

---

## Template Structure

Each case study uses the following sections. Not all sections will be equally developed for every case — use judgment about what the situation makes visible and what it doesn't.

---

### [Case Title]

*One sentence describing the situation.*

**Date / timeframe:**
**Domain / context:**
**Primary Stack entry point:** *(Which layer is the most natural starting point?)*

---

#### Situation

2–4 paragraphs describing what happened. Factual and concrete. No Stack vocabulary yet. What were the conditions? Who were the actors? What did they do? What were the consequences?

---

#### Layer Analysis

For each layer: one or two paragraphs on how this layer was engaged, stressed, or illuminated by the situation. Not all six layers need equal treatment — weight them by relevance.

**L1 — Material Base:** What material conditions shaped this situation? Who controlled what?

**L2 — Commons & Institutions:** What governance structures were present? How did they function or fail?

**L3 — Legibility & Metrics:** What could be seen? By whom? What was invisible, contested, or misrepresented?

**L4 — Decision Systems & Authority:** How were binding decisions made? Were they contestable? What was irreversible?

**L5 — Knowledge & Intelligence:** How did collective understanding form? Was it reliable, fragmented, or adversarially disrupted?

**L6 — Human Capacity & Care:** What relational conditions were present? What sustained or depleted them?

---

#### Patterns Present

For each relevant pattern: name it, note how it appeared in this situation, and mark whether this case confirms or strains the pattern description.

Format:
**[Pattern Name]** — How it appeared. *Confirms / Strains / Extends* the pattern description. *(Strain or extension notes if applicable.)*

---

#### Provocations Alive in This Situation

Which of the eight provocations are most active here? For each: name it and note specifically how it manifests.

---

#### What This Case Illuminates

What does this case reveal that the Stack's current architecture handles well? What does it reveal that the architecture struggles with? Is there a dynamic present here that the existing patterns and layers don't yet name cleanly?

---

#### What Remains Open

What questions does this case raise that the Stack cannot currently answer? What would the Stack need to develop in order to address them?

---

*Case studies are analytical, not prescriptive. They do not argue for a specific intervention or outcome. Their function is to make the Stack's diagnostic capacity visible by applying it to a concrete situation.*
