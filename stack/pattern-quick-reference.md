# Pattern Quick Reference

*All 20 patterns — one or two sentences each. Use this for rapid orientation when reading or writing. Full pattern documents are in [/patterns/](../patterns/).*

*Primary layer codes: L1 Material Base · L2 Commons & Institutions · L3 Legibility & Metrics · L4 Decision Systems & Authority · L5 Knowledge & Intelligence · L6 Human Capacity & Care*

---

## Tier 1 — Foundational

**[Algorithmic Authority](../patterns/algorithmic-authority.md)**
Automated systems accumulate decision-making power by mimicking institutional authority without inheriting its accountability structures. The mechanism: opacity + scale removes the friction through which contestation normally functions.
*Primary layers: L4, L3*

**[Epistemic Warfare](../patterns/epistemic-warfare.md)**
The systematic dismantling of shared factual baselines to render collective accountability structurally impossible. Not disinformation — the destruction of the *conditions* under which true and false can be reliably distinguished together.
*Primary layers: L5, L4*

**[Legitimacy Drift](../patterns/legitimacy-drift.md)**
Authority surrenders the need for public consent and replaces it with structural control of information and enforcement. Happens gradually through accumulated small decisions, visible in retrospect.
*Primary layers: L4, L3*

---

## Tier 2 — Structural Dynamics

**[Care vs. Coercion](../patterns/care-vs-coercion.md)**
Relations are governed by control masquerading as protection, replacing mutual accountability with dependency and binding agency through the language of safety. The authority claimed is care; the mechanism is leverage over need.
*Primary layers: L6, L4*

**[Platform Capture](../patterns/platform-capture.md)**
Private infrastructure absorbs public governance functions while evading corresponding public accountability mechanisms. Capture occurs gradually, by the infrastructure becoming essential before the governance implications are visible.
*Primary layers: L2, L4*

**[Obedience in Advance](../patterns/obedience-in-advance.md)**
Individuals and institutions preemptively align with anticipated power demands, constraining their own agency to avoid explicit coercion. Self-censorship and institutional capitulation as the primary mechanism — no direct pressure required.
*Primary layers: L6, L4*

**[Institutional Weaponization](../patterns/institutional-weaponization.md)**
Institutions transform from neutral arbitration mechanisms into offensive tools deployed against political opponents. The institution's formal legitimacy is the weapon: processes designed for accountability become instruments of harassment or capture.
*Primary layers: L4, L2*

---

## Tier 3 — Extended Terrain

**[Manufactured Ambiguity](../patterns/manufactured-ambiguity.md)**
Ambiguity is weaponized to obscure intent, erode accountability, and exhaust the capacity for collective action. Unlike strategic ambiguity, its function is not optionality but the elimination of the conditions for effective contestation.
*Primary layers: L3, L4*

**[Strategic Ambiguity](../patterns/strategic-ambiguity.md)**
Authority intentionally obscures intent to preserve optionality and suppress substantive contestation. Deliberate — ambiguity functions as governance tool rather than communication failure.
*Primary layers: L4, L3*

**[Commons Destruction](../patterns/commons-destruction.md)**
Shared resources, institutions, and relational infrastructure are systematically enclosed, privatized, or degraded to concentrate agency. The destruction of the commons is not a side effect of development — it is often the mechanism through which authority is consolidated.
*Primary layers: L2, L1*

**[Material Base Sovereignty](../patterns/material-base-sovereignty.md)**
Control over food, energy, and logistics dictates the conditions for agency; whoever secures the material substrate defines the boundaries of all political action. Material control converts into political constraint without requiring explicit coercion.
*Primary layers: L1, L4*

**[Distributed Resistance](../patterns/distributed-resistance.md)**
Collective agency persists against suppression by dispersing leadership and infrastructure across decentralized networks that lack a single point of failure. Coordination without central command — not leaderlessness but distributed architecture.
*Primary layers: L2, L6*

---

## Procedural Dynamics

**[Proceduralism as Shield](../patterns/proceduralism-as-shield.md)**
Process is deployed as the primary defense against substantive challenge, absorbing contestation without enabling accountability. The form of accountability is satisfied; the substance is not. Challenge is routed into procedure; procedure is satisfied; substantive review never occurs.
*Primary layers: L4, L3*

**[Procedural Entombment](../patterns/procedural-entombment.md)**
Process crystallizes into permanent form, immune to revision, converting the mechanisms of accountability into the substance of permanence. The most insidious form: the rule that must be followed in order to change the rule.
*Primary layers: L4, L2*

---

## Collective Governance

**[Collective Leadership Architecture](../patterns/collective-leadership-architecture.md)**
Authority distributes across roles, protocols, and capacities rather than persons, enabling coherent collective action without a single point of capture or failure. A design pattern, not a failure mode — describes how distributed leadership is held structurally rather than personally.
*Primary layers: L2, L4*

---

## Epistemic & Care

**[Semantic Capture](../patterns/semantic-capture.md)**
Governance vocabulary is colonized by technical or market language, narrowing what can be thought and contested before any formal decision is made. Argument within the captured vocabulary reproduces the assumptions of those who set the terms.
*Primary layers: L3, L5*

**[Care Infrastructure Failure](../patterns/care-infrastructure-failure.md)**
Care-based systems collapse not because their design was wrong but because the material conditions that would make them work have been systematically destroyed. The demand for care is generated by the same conditions that eliminate care capacity.
*Primary layers: L6, L1*

---

## Previously Established

**[Temporal Capture](../patterns/temporal-capture.md)**
The future is endlessly promised while present conditions deteriorate, delaying accountability and suppressing action. The gap between promise and delivery is the space in which authority extracts without obligation.
*Primary layers: L4, L3*

**[Internalized Policing](../patterns/internal-policing.md)**
Social norms and enforcement are crowdsourced to peers, reducing the need for overt institutional coercion. Compliance maintained through social threat, not institutional force — more durable than external coercion because it generates no visible oppressor.
*Primary layers: L6, L4*

**[Hybrid War](../patterns/hybrid-war.md)**
A mode of conflict that degrades authority and coherence across multiple domains without crossing formal thresholds of war. Operates simultaneously across economic, informational, institutional, and material dimensions — each action individually deniable, collectively decisive.
*Primary layers: L4, L5, L3*
