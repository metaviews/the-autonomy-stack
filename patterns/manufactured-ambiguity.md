# Pattern: Manufactured Ambiguity

## Summary
Ambiguity is weaponized to obscure intent, erode accountability, and exhaust the capacity for collective action.

## Core Dynamic
Power operates by embedding opacity in communication and procedure to deflect blame while retaining operational leverage.

## Signals
- Coded messaging legible only to insiders while obscuring intent from oversight.
- Outgoing actions delegated to proxies to maintain formal deniability.
- Procedural delays masked as administrative friction or standard protocol.
- Criticism met with irony or claims of misunderstanding rather than engagement.
- Conflicting data releases that prevent consensus on factual reality.

## What It Targets
Collective capacity to verify facts, coordinate action, and hold power to account.

## Why It Works
It exploits the high social cost of clarity, making those who demand it appear disruptive, humorless, or paranoid.

## Common Misreadings
- "This is just bureaucratic inefficiency."
- "You're reading too much into it."
- "They didn't mean it that way."
- "We just need more time to clarify."
