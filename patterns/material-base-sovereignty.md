# Pattern: Material Base Sovereignty

## Summary
Control over food, energy, and logistics dictates the conditions for agency; whoever secures the material substrate defines the boundaries of all political action.

## Core Dynamic
Power operates by externalizing essential survival systems, converting technical dependency into a mechanism of control that renders legal authority secondary to physical capacity.

## Signals
- Critical infrastructure relies on single private vendors with no public exit option.
- Dissent is suppressed by cutting access to networks rather than arresting individuals.
- Governance decisions are framed as technical or neutral, obscuring who controls the physical constraints.
- Survival depends on building parallel logistics rather than relying on public provisioning.
- Legal rights exist on paper but cannot be enacted when material channels are blocked.
- Resource scarcity (energy, bandwidth) is treated as a neutral market signal rather than a political choice.

## What It Targets
It erodes the capacity for non-compliant agency by binding survival directly to the will of those controlling the infrastructure.

## Why It Works
It bypasses ideological debate by making resistance materially impossible, leveraging the biological imperative of survival over the political appeal of liberty.

## Common Misreadings
- "That is just neutral infrastructure."
- "Security requires centralized control."
- "Market efficiency delivers the best outcome for everyone."
- "Policy can regulate the private sector if needed."
