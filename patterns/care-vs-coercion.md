# Pattern: Care vs. Coercion

## Summary
Relations are governed by control masquerading as protection, replacing mutual accountability with dependency and binding agency through the language of safety.

## Core Dynamic
Power operates by framing compliance as safety and dissent as risk, making resistance appear unfaithful to collective well-being.

## Signals
- Rhetoric of survival or retribution replacing policy debate
- Ethical pledges coinciding with centralized system consolidation
- Economic leverage used to suppress dissent under stability banners
- Systems designed to remove human friction and judgment
- Fear of reporting failures leading to operational blindness
- Infrastructure dependency used to bypass legal authority

## What It Targets
It targets the relational capacity for trust and the ability to critique without the risk of exclusion.

## Why It Works
It exploits the human need for security to normalize control, making dissent feel like a threat to collective stability.

## Common Misreadings
- "This is necessary protection"
- "Efficiency justifies the oversight"
- "Trustless technology removes human error"
- "Stability is worth the cost of control"
