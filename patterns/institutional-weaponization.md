# Pattern: Institutional Weaponization

## Summary
Institutions transform from neutral arbitration mechanisms into offensive tools deployed against political opponents.

## Core Dynamic
Administrative neutrality collapses as bureaucratic machinery is redirected from public service to partisan enforcement.

## Signals
- Civil service recruitment prioritizes political loyalty over statutory competence
- Regulatory oversight is selectively applied to opponents while allies receive exemptions
- Administrative agencies are restructured under mandates of "efficiency" to remove institutional memory
- Official channels frame lawful investigations as political persecution or coordinated plots
- Surveillance tools designed for public safety are repurposed to monitor political rivals

## What It Targets
It erodes the shared trust in institutional neutrality required for voluntary compliance and collective governance.

## Why It Works
It concentrates power by monopolizing the definition of legality, forcing opponents to expend resources defending their status rather than building alternatives.

## Common Misreadings
- "This is just normal political turnover"
- "They are cleaning up an unresponsive bureaucracy"
- "We just need leadership aligned with the mandate"
- "It is standard partisan rotation of power"
