# Pattern: Legitimacy Drift

## Summary
Authority surrenders the need for public consent and replaces it with structural control of information and enforcement.

## Core Dynamic
Gradual departures from accountability exhaust the legitimacy deficit until force becomes the primary mechanism of compliance.

## Signals
- Routine administrative data is classified or redacted without public justification
- Oversight bodies have standing but lack access to necessary information
- Criticism is treated as operational security risk rather than policy dispute
- Delay becomes the primary method for resolving substantive disputes
- Transparency is framed as a vulnerability rather than a baseline requirement

## What It Targets
The collective ability to distinguish lawful authority from coercive power.

## Why It Works
It exhausts the opposition through procedural friction before they can identify the specific point of failure.

## Common Misreadings
- "This is just necessary security protocol"
- "They need time to explain the reasoning"
- "Oversight exists, so the system is safe"
- "Transparency is a technical gap, not a policy choice"
