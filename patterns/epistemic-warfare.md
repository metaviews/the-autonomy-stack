# Pattern: Epistemic Warfare

## Summary
The systematic dismantling of shared factual baselines to render collective accountability structurally impossible.

## Core Dynamic
Power maintains itself by severing the link between observation and reality, replacing verification with emotional resonance and authority-based validation.

## Signals
- Institutional trust erodes faster than the material crisis escalates
- Algorithms prioritize engagement metrics over truth verification
- Administrative machinery repurposes from neutral service to loyalty enforcement
- History and source records obscured by generative AI summaries
- Confusion deployed as a strategic control tool rather than accidental noise
- Transparency metrics show availability without access or legibility

## What It Targets
Legibility, shared situational awareness, and the knowledge infrastructure required to exercise and contest authority.

## Why It Works
It exhausts the capacity for verification by making shared reality too expensive to maintain, isolating individuals in unverifiable tribal realities.

## Common Misreadings
- "It's just information overload"
- "They are just spreading lies"
- "We need better transparency"
- "It's inevitable polarization"
