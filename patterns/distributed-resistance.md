# Pattern: Distributed Resistance

## Summary
Collective agency persists against suppression by dispersing leadership and infrastructure across decentralized networks that lack a single point of failure.

## Core Dynamic
Authority functions through redundancy and mutual aid rather than hierarchy, forcing opponents to dismantle the entire network to stop a single node.

## Signals
- Leadership structures explicitly refuse named figureheads or central command
- Communication protocols shift to offline or encrypted layers when digital channels are severed
- Information validation relies on peer verification rather than institutional credentials
- Infrastructure operates as redundant systems parallel to official channels
- Coordination emerges horizontally without top-down mandates

## What It Targets
The concentration of authority, eroding the ability of powerholders to neutralize resistance by removing key individuals.

## Why It Works
Removing the head removes the lever of control, rendering decapitation strategies ineffective against fluid organizational forms.

## Common Misreadings
- "This is just disorganization without cohesion"
- "They are operating without accountability"
- "Coordination requires central command"
- "They can't win without a unified front"
