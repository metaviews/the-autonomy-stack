# Pattern: Platform Capture

## Summary
Private infrastructure absorbs public governance functions while evading corresponding public accountability mechanisms.

## Core Dynamic
Control over essential communication and decision architecture allows operators to set binding rules and allocate resources without facing the legal or political constraints that limit state authority.

## Signals
- Critical services are offered on terms set unilaterally by private architects
- Algorithms resolve disputes or allocate resources without human review
- Policy choices are described as technical constraints rather than political decisions
- Engagement signals replace verification or expertise as the basis of authority
- Public infrastructure relies on narrow vendor sets with no exit options
- Government security mandates absorb private innovation into state power

## What It Targets
The capacity to contest the conditions of interaction and distribution, collapsing the distinction between private interest and public good.

## Why It Works
Indispensability creates leverage; resistance is framed as obstruction of progress or security rather than a democratic choice.

## Common Misreadings
- "Technology is neutral."
- "It's just efficiency."
- "We are not governing."
- "The market will correct this."
